+01How GO works+
The model

Post it, they book it, you earn.

Two content types carry the program: accommodation and things to do. Everything else — your numbers, your level, your rewards — is computed from what those posts actually sell.

ACC

Accommodation content

Hotels and stays. The app tracks your accommodation posts separately because they carry the higher commission, and it keeps an ACC streak so consistency is visible.

TTD

Things-to-do content

Tours, attractions and activities. Counted alongside your accommodation posts, so a month can be built from either side or both.

Commission

Paid on orders, not posts

You earn commission on the orders your content drives. GMV, orders and redeemed orders all show up in the app so you can see which posts actually converted.
