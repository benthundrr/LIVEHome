What Thundrr offers creators

Two things come with joining Thundrr: a dedicated person on your account and the THUNDRR LIVE app.

  • Dedicated creator manager Every creator gets a dedicated creator manager who knows your schedule and numbers, one tap away in app messages.
  • LIVE coaching and THUNDRR AI Coaching on scheduling, LIVE Battles, and retention, plus THUNDRR AI and LIVE Guides inside the app.
  • THUNDRR LIVE app A personal dashboard for Diamonds, LIVE hours, and active days, alongside LIVE recaps, leaderboards, tasks, badges, a rewards store, and milestone merch.
  • Battles, tournaments, and LIVE Fest Thundrr-hosted LIVE Battles and tournaments, plus planning and progress tracking for TikTok LIVE Fest and regional events.
  • Violation and account support Strikes, takedowns, bans, and account restrictions are raised with TikTok with a manager following up on your case.
  • Regional support Teams operating in the United States, Latin America, the United Kingdom, and Australia across English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Italian.

Terms and verification

Joining Thundrr is free: no joining fee, no cut of your Diamonds, and no contract. Applications are reviewed by hand, and invitations arrive inside the TikTok app through Agency Center.

  1. Apply online Submit your handle through thundrr.co/join and select your region.
  2. Confirm the TikTok handle @thundrr.co is Thundrr’s only TikTok LIVE account. Check that the invitation in Agency Center matches before accepting.
  3. Intro call and onboarding Meet your creator manager, connect inside the THUNDRR LIVE app, and set a LIVE schedule.
