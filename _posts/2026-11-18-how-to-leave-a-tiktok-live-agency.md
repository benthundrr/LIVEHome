## How TikTok links you to an agency

On TikTok, a LIVE agency is called a Creator Network. TikTok describes Creator Networks as [third parties independent from TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide) that support creators with LIVE operations, guidance and resources.

You join one inside the TikTok app. TikTok's [agency invite page](https://www.tiktok.com/tcn/agency_invite) says an agent uses your invitation code to send you an invitation, and you read and accept it.

- **Agency Center** Your invitation code is under Settings and privacy, Creator tools, LIVE Center, Agency Center.
- **The invitation** The network sends it using your code, and you accept it in the app.
- **Your join date** TikTok counts the later of the day you accepted or the date named in the invitation.
- **One network** TikTok's policy says networks shouldn't invite a creator who has already joined another network.
{: .cards}

The join date and one-network rules come from TikTok's Creator Network Multi-Account Violation Policy, which TikTok publishes for networks on LIVE Backstage. It says TikTok expects one eligible creator to use one TikTok account to join one Creator Network.

## Before you request to leave

Leaving starts with knowing what you agreed to. TikTok's Creator Network Management Policy, its rulebook for networks on LIVE Backstage, refers to creators' agreements with Creator Networks, so your network may have its own terms with you.

TikTok's Creator Networks site says networks can [manage contracts](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en) in LIVE Backstage, the platform TikTok provides to networks.

### What to check first

- **Your agreement** Read any terms you accepted with your network, including notice or exit terms.
- **Agency Center** Open it in the TikTok app and see what it shows for your account right now.
- **Your agency** Tell your manager you're thinking of leaving and ask what the process looks like for you.
{: .cards}

> **Tip** Screenshot what your agreement and Agency Center say before you act. Your own app is the final word on your account.
{: .callout .tip}

### About the numbers you see online

You'll see different notice periods and waiting times quoted online. We couldn't find a public TikTok page that lists a notice period for leaving or says how long the cooling-down period lasts, so we don't quote one here.

TikTok's policies also say they can change over time. If a TikTok notice in your app gives you a date or a deadline, go by that.

## How switching to a new network works

Switching means leaving your current network and then joining a new one through a fresh invitation. You can't be in two at once.

TikTok's Multi-Account Violation Policy lists creators that networks should not invite. That includes creators who have already joined a Creator Network, and creators who left their last network but are still in the cooling-down period.

1. **Settle things with your current network** Check your agreement and Agency Center, and talk to your agency.
2. **Wait until you can be invited** While you're still in a network or in a cooling-down period, other networks can't invite you.
3. **Share your invitation code** Get your code from Agency Center and give it to the new network.
4. **Read and accept the invitation** Check that it comes from the network you chose, then accept it in the app.
{: .steps}

## Don't switch with a second account

Some creators try to skip the wait by joining a new network on another account. TikTok's policy calls this a multi-account violation.

It says a creator who is already in a network, or isn't eligible to join one, must not use another TikTok account to join a different network. The policy also covers going LIVE stably on another account you open or borrow while you're in a network.

| If TikTok finds a multi-account violation | What its policy says can happen |
|---|---|
| The second account joined a new network | TikTok can remove it from that network |
| The creator was already in a network | TikTok can move the second account to the original network |
| Creators want to dispute it | Networks and creators can appeal after the in-app notification |
| No appeal is raised in time | TikTok treats the violation as recognized |

TikTok says it decides who uses an account based on who appears most in its recent LIVE and short video content. A new account with you on camera is still you.

> **Heads up** If a network tells you to open a new account to join them, walk away. TikTok's Management Policy bars networks from encouraging ineligible creators to open alternative accounts to join.
{: .callout .warn}

## What a good network does when you leave

TikTok's Management Policy sets rules for how networks treat creators. It says a network should treat the creators it manages honestly and fairly, and protect their personal information.

It also bars networks from failing to resolve disputes with creators they manage or used to manage in an appropriate manner. And it bars poaching creators from other networks by inappropriate means, such as soliciting them to breach their agreements in an inappropriate manner.
