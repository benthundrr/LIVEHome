## The short answer

Your LIVE Fest 2026 rank is on the campaign page in the TikTok app. That page shows the track TikTok placed you in, the day-by-day dates for each round, and the leaderboards you're on.

Power League runs November 20 to December 6, 2026, on TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest). TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, while TikTok Japan's November 13 [press announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6.

- **Where** The LIVE Fest campaign page in the TikTok app
- **Which board** Your own round 1 track first, then the overall Power League board
- **When it's final** After TikTok confirms totals at the end of each module
- **Who advances** A number per track, shown on your campaign page
{: .cards}

## Where to see your LIVE Fest rank

Start in the TikTok app. Find LIVE Fest in the app, or start from [tiktok.com/livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest), and open the campaign page.

1. **Update the TikTok app** TikTok's Event Rules say an older app version may not collect points.
2. **Open the campaign page** Find LIVE Fest in the TikTok app and open your page.
3. **Read your track** TikTok's Event Rules place creators in the same region into round 1 tracks by their highest single-month Diamonds over a six-month window. Your page shows the track you were given.
4. **Check your round dates** Power League has three rounds, and the day-by-day calendar is in the app only.
5. **Find your board** Look at your track's leaderboard before the overall one.
{: .steps}

Your track's board is the one that decides round 1. TikTok places you with creators of a similar monthly Diamond total, so where you stand there matters more than where you'd sit on a list of every creator in your region.

There's also an overall Power League leaderboard. TikTok's Event Rules say the early points from signing up with I'm in carry into it one-to-one. Confirm on your campaign page.

> **Tip** Screenshot your track board and your round dates at the start of each round. It makes it easier to spot a change once TikTok confirms the totals.
{: .callout .tip}

## How to tell if you advanced

TikTok's Power League one-pager in the app describes three rounds. How many creators move on from round 1 depends on the track, and TikTok hasn't published one cutoff for everyone. Your campaign page shows the number for your track.

| Round | What TikTok describes | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | You compete in the track TikTok placed you in | Your track's advance number and your place on its board |
| Round 2 | Creators who advance merge into three tracks: Legend, Crown and Rising | Which of the three tracks you landed in |
| Round 3 | The same three tracks continue, and top creators advance to the global finals | Your place on your track's board |

Don't read the board at the last minute of a round as your result. TikTok's Event Rules say the points shown on a leaderboard may contain errors, and TikTok confirms the final totals after each module ends. Check your campaign page again once the round has closed.

TikTok hasn't published how round 1 tracks map onto Legend, Crown and Rising. If you advanced, your campaign page is the place that shows which track you moved into.

> **Heads up** A post or account that lists an exact advance cutoff for every track is guessing. TikTok shows the number for your track on your campaign page.
{: .callout .warn}

## If you made the next round

A few things change once you're in round 2. Confirm each one on your campaign page.

- **Carry-over** TikTok's Event Rules say creators carry 5% of their points from one round to the next, up to a cap that varies by track.
- **Match bonus** In rounds 2 and 3, your highest-scoring LIVE match of the day is added to your points one-to-one.
- **Same bonus windows** Bonus Hour is a daily 2x window, so confirm your local hour in the app. Double Blast gives 2x for 30 minutes once a day and can't run during Bonus Hour.
- **Votes** Votes count only when a viewer sends them by hand, at 1 Vote = 1 point.
{: .cards}

The carry-over is small, so a new round is close to a fresh start. Plan your strongest LIVEs for the first days of the round instead of waiting for the end.

The full round structure is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) guide. Timing tips for each bonus are in [Double Blast and Bonus Hour explained](/blog/live-fest-2026-double-blast-bonus-hour/) and [how to get more points in TikTok LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/how-to-get-more-points-live-fest/).

## If you didn't advance

Missing a Power League round doesn't end your LIVE Fest. TikTok's LIVE Fest page lists Fandom League from December 9 to 15, with Talent League in the same block, and the global finals from December 17 to 21.

TikTok's Event Rules say one creator can be listed on up to three global finals boards, one track from each league. So a creator who doesn't advance in Power League can still aim for a Fandom League or Talent League board. Confirm on your campaign page.

- **Fandom League** TikTok places you in one of six tracks by your number of active fans, as of a date on your campaign page. See the [Fandom League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/).
- **Talent League** Music, Dancing or Gaming, each with its own audition line. See the [Talent League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/).
- **Your viewers' Votes** TikTok's rules say unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, so they can still count in the later leagues.
- **Your rewards** Virtual rewards go to your account after the module ends, and physical rewards come with claim instructions in the app.
{: .cards}

Crossover League isn't on TikTok's public schedule, so check your campaign page for its dates. It's covered in the [Crossover League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-crossover-league/), and what happens when Power League closes is in [what happens after LIVE Fest Power League ends](/blog/after-live-fest-power-league/).

## When your rank looks wrong

Most rank surprises have a reason in TikTok's own rules. Check these before you assume a mistake.

- **The module hasn't closed** TikTok confirms final totals after each module ends.
- **An old app version** TikTok's rules say an older version may not collect points.
- **A hidden or deleted video** Votes earned by a video are deducted if that video is removed or set to private before the campaign ends.
- **Opting out** Creators who opt out lose their points and rankings from the boards until they rejoin.
- **Rule breaks** A VPN, a second account, bots or fake engagement can get you removed.
{: .cards}

If something still looks wrong after the module closes, contact TikTok through the app. Every reason points go missing is in [LIVE Fest points not counting?](/blog/live-fest-points-not-counting/), and who can take part is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/). Every published date is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

> Your campaign page is the only scoreboard that counts. Check it after each round closes, then plan the next one.
