## Why creators worry about agency scams

TikTok LIVE agencies are real. TikTok calls them Creator Networks and describes them as "third parties independent from TikTok" that support creators with livestream operations, education and development guidance.

The worry comes from the messages creators get. Anyone can send a DM that says "agency," and threads asking whether an invitation is a scam come up again and again on Reddit.

> **Heads up** TikTok says Creator Networks operate on their own behalf, and it doesn't endorse or sponsor them. Be cautious of anyone who claims to be part of TikTok.
{: .callout .warn}

## Red flags to watch for

- **A fee** A deposit, joining fee or verification payment. A legitimate network shouldn't charge you to join.
- **Your password or codes** TikTok says it [will never ask for your account details](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/avoid-fraudulent-message-attacks-on-tiktok), and a real network doesn't need them.
- **Off-app links** Forms, group chats or websites that replace TikTok's own invitation flow.
- **Guaranteed income or viewers** No network controls TikTok's rewards or recommendations, so a promise like that is a warning sign.
{: .cards}

TikTok's Help Center also warns against third-party sites that promise free likes, fans, Coins or other incentives, because they may be able to get your login information.

## How a real invitation works

TikTok's [agency invite page](https://www.tiktok.com/tcn/agency_invite) lays out the in-app flow. An agent uses your invitation code to send you an invitation, and you read and accept it in the app.

1. **Find your code** Go to Settings and privacy, Creator tools, LIVE Center, Agency Center.
2. **Share it with the agency** The agency uses your code to send an invitation from its side.
3. **Read the invitation** Check that it comes from the network you've been talking to.
4. **Accept or decline** You decide in the app. Nothing needs to happen outside TikTok.
{: .steps}

In its [post on agency invitations](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-agency-invitation), TikTok says creators who get one "will have an opportunity to learn more about the Creator Network, understand the collaboration, and decide whether to continue the onboarding process."

## What to ask before you accept

### What does it cost?

Ask whether there's any fee and whether the agency takes any share of your Diamonds. Get the answer in writing.

### Is there a contract?

Ask how long any agreement lasts, whether there are minimum LIVE hours, and how you can leave.

### Who will support me?

Ask who your contact will be and how you'll reach them. At Thundrr, every creator gets a dedicated creator manager.
