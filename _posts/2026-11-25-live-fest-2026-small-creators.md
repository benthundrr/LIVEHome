## The short answer

For most small creators, LIVE Fest is worth joining. It's free, TikTok starts you next to creators of a similar size, and the campaign gives your viewers a clear reason to show up and support you.

It's not worth it if you treat it as income or burn out chasing a rank. TikTok runs the leaderboards, prizes go to top creators in each track, and nobody is guaranteed one.

- **Cost to join** Free, through the campaign page in the TikTok app
- **Who you start against** Creators in a similar Diamond range, in Power League round 1
- **What fans can do for free** Collect Votes from missions and send them to you
- **What's not guaranteed** A rank, a prize or an event invitation
{: .cards}

## Who can join LIVE Fest 2026

TikTok's Event Rules set who can take part. You need to be 18 or older (19 in Korea), have LIVE access, and be in a listed region, which includes the United States and Puerto Rico. Business, organization, government and e-commerce promotion accounts aren't eligible, and using a VPN can get you removed.

If you can't go LIVE yet, start with TikTok's [LIVE requirements](/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/). The full eligibility list is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/), and US details are in [LIVE Fest 2026 for US creators](/blog/live-fest-2026-us-creators/).

## How TikTok keeps small creators out of the big leagues early

The fear most small creators have is being ranked next to accounts with huge audiences. TikTok's Event Rules handle that at the start of Power League.

TikTok places creators in the same region into round 1 tracks by the highest number of Diamonds they collected in any single month over a six-month window. A creator with a small monthly total starts in a track with others like them. Your campaign page shows the track you were given.

Creators who advance from round 1 merge into three tracks, Legend, Crown and Rising, for rounds 2 and 3. TikTok's [Las Vegas recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) lists Power League Rising Track among the seven LIVE Fest 2025 categories, each with its own winner. How round 1 tracks feed the later tracks isn't public, so read [the Power League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) and check your campaign page.

> **Tip** Your round 1 track is the board that matters first. Watch where you stand there, not on the overall leaderboard.
{: .callout .tip}

## Which league fits a smaller account

LIVE Fest 2026 has more than one way in. Each league ranks something different, so pick the one that matches what you already do.

| League | Who it suits | How TikTok groups or qualifies you |
|---|---|---|
| Power League | Any LIVE creator | Round 1 tracks by highest single-month Diamonds over six months |
| Fandom League | Creators with a loyal regular crowd | Tracks assigned by active fans |
| Talent League | Music, dance and gaming creators | Audition line per track |
| Crossover League | Short-video creators who also go LIVE | Levels, with a global board from Level 3 |

Talent League has its own lines. TikTok's Talent video says Music is for music-certified creators, Dancing asks for 3 dance LIVEs in the past 7 days with at least 5% dance content, and Gaming asks for at least 50% gaming content across 14 days. Details are in [the Talent League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/).

Crossover League rewards short videos and LIVEs together. TikTok's @tiktok_livefest account says creators post short videos, go LIVE and grow their community to level up. If your videos get more reach than your LIVEs, [the Crossover League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-crossover-league/) is worth a read.

Fandom League runs December 9 to 15 and assigns tracks by active fans, so a small but loyal crowd counts. See [the Fandom League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/).

## What a small creator can realistically get

Be honest with yourself about the goal. For most small creators, the real wins come from the campaign itself, not the trophy.

- **More reasons to go LIVE** Round dates and daily bonuses give you a plan for the week.
- **A reason for viewers to come back** Fans have Votes to send and a board to watch.
- **Free support from fans** TikTok's Event Rules say Votes come from missions and count 1 Vote = 1 point.
- **In-app rewards for top creators** TikTok's in-app rules describe effects, badges and frames, swag, trophies and Diamonds for top creators in each track.
{: .cards}

The bigger honors are for the top of each board. TikTok's LIVE Fest page says the global finals, December 17 to 21, crown the Creator of 2026, and TikTok says the campaign ends with a gala in Tokyo in 2027. The in-app rules say nobody is guaranteed an invitation. What each reward means is in [LIVE Fest 2026 prizes and rewards](/blog/live-fest-2026-prizes-rewards/).

TikTok is also bringing back the LIVE Fest Creator Academy in the United States, Vietnam, Indonesia and Latin America, with creator-led LIVE sessions to help creators prepare. That's useful at any size.

## When LIVE Fest isn't worth it

LIVE Fest can work against you if it changes how you treat your viewers. Skip the push, or scale it back, if any of these sound like you.

- You'd need to go LIVE far more than you can keep up after December.
- You'd be asking the same few viewers to spend more than they want to.
- You're counting on prizes or Diamonds to pay bills.
- You'd bend TikTok's rules to climb, which can get you removed. See [TikTok LIVE bans and restrictions](/blog/tiktok-live-ban-restriction/).

> **Heads up** Anyone who promises you a LIVE Fest rank, a prize or a guaranteed spot is guessing. TikTok runs the leaderboards.
{: .callout .warn}

## How to make LIVE Fest work with a small audience

A smaller account can still have a good LIVE Fest. These steps keep the effort in proportion.

1. **Sign up and read your campaign page** Check your league, track and round dates. Every date is in [the LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).
2. **Pick a steady schedule** A few set times a week that your regulars can plan around beats long random streams.
3. **Teach your viewers to vote** Share [how fans vote in LIVE Fest](/blog/live-fest-vote-how-fans-support-creators/) so they know Votes are free to collect and must be sent by hand.
4. **Use the bonuses on your best days** Save Double Blast for when your regulars are on. See [Double Blast and Bonus Hour](/blog/live-fest-2026-double-blast-bonus-hour/).
5. **Grow while you compete** New viewers who find you during the campaign can stay after it ends. Read [how to get more viewers on TikTok LIVE](/blog/how-to-get-more-viewers-on-tiktok-live/).
{: .steps}
