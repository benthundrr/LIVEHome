## The short answer

A TikTok LIVE stream key is a code TikTok gives you, together with a server URL, so streaming software on your computer can send video to your LIVE. You get one when you set up a LIVE on desktop, and you need a new one for every LIVE.

- **What it is** A stream key plus a server URL (TikTok also calls it the RTMP URL) that you paste into software such as OBS
- **Who gets one** Creators with LIVE access whose account offers desktop LIVE. TikTok says desktop options may vary by account and region
- **Where it is** On TikTok's Go LIVE setup on a desktop browser, shown after you start setting up a LIVE
- **How long it lasts** One LIVE. TikTok says keys expire after a period of inactivity
{: .cards}

Everything on this page comes from TikTok's own help, Creator Academy and creator pages. If a detail isn't on those pages, we say so.

## Who can get a TikTok stream key

A stream key is only useful once you can go LIVE. TikTok's Help Center article [What is TikTok LIVE?](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7543604790438451768) lists the rules for going LIVE at all.

| What TikTok publishes | What it says |
|---|---|
| Age to go LIVE | At least 18 |
| Age to send or receive Gifts | At least 18, or 19 in South Korea |
| Followers to go LIVE | 1,000, which may vary across regions |
| Where LIVE works | LIVE and Gift features aren't currently available everywhere |
| Desktop LIVE options | TikTok's [guide to going LIVE](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok) says availability may vary by account and region |

TikTok doesn't publish a separate follower count, age or form for stream keys. The only reliable check is your own account: log in on a computer and see whether TikTok offers you a desktop LIVE with a key.

> **Heads up** Be careful with anyone selling stream keys or offering to unlock one in exchange for your TikTok login. TikTok's pages don't describe any way to buy a key, and your password should stay with you.
{: .callout .warn}

## Where to find your TikTok stream key

TikTok's Creator Academy article [TikTok Live 101](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/live-best-practices-publishers) gives the desktop steps under Desktop Formats via Stream Key. TikTok's guide to going LIVE describes the first step as selecting the available Go LIVE or LIVE option.

1. **Log in on desktop** Open TikTok in a desktop browser, log in and click Go LIVE.
2. **Add a title** Enter a title of up to 32 characters.
3. **Keep the LIVE open** Select the option to prevent the LIVE from ending automatically, and start.
4. **Copy the key** Copy the stream key and the RTMP URL, then set them up in your streaming software.
5. **Start from your software** TikTok says your LIVE only becomes visible to viewers once you start the broadcast from your streaming software.
{: .steps}

TikTok's in-app page on connecting to a computer says the server URL and stream key sit in the settings of your LIVE broadcast. It also says every new LIVE broadcast needs a new server URL and stream key, so a key you saved from last week won't carry over.

### Putting the key into OBS

TikTok's OBS steps are short. Open OBS and pick or create your scene, add your sources such as a webcam, microphone or game capture, then enter the Server URL and Stream Key TikTok gave you in OBS's streaming settings. Check your video and audio in the preview, then click Start Streaming.

Copy the server URL TikTok shows you each time. TikTok doesn't publish a fixed server address, so don't reuse one from a tutorial.

## TikTok LIVE Studio vs OBS

LIVE Studio is TikTok's own desktop app for going LIVE. TikTok's [LIVE Studio FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/FAQ/FAQ) says it and OBS have similar functions, but LIVE Studio is developed by TikTok, you log in with your TikTok account, and it offers better information and content safety for creators.

| | TikTok LIVE Studio | OBS with a stream key |
|---|---|---|
| Made by | TikTok | A separate streaming app |
| How it connects | You log in with your TikTok account | You paste the stream key and server URL TikTok gives you |
| Computers | Windows and macOS, per TikTok's LIVE Studio help | Whatever your OBS install supports |
| Access | Its own LIVE access check inside LIVE Studio | Depends on whether your account offers desktop LIVE with a key |
| New key each LIVE | No key to paste | Yes, every new LIVE needs a new key |

### How LIVE Studio access works

TikTok's LIVE Studio help page [Apply for LIVE access](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Before-you-go-LIVE/Apply-for-LIVE-access) says you check your status in the top right corner of LIVE Studio, under Your LIVE access. Active LIVE creators can get access when their account meets requirements on account age, recent LIVE time, followers and no recent suspension or revocation.

TikTok doesn't publish the numbers. It says the requirements vary by country or region and are subject to change without notice, so LIVE Studio shows you what you still need. Streamers coming from other platforms can apply with a form about their channel there, and TikTok says the review might take days.

> **Tip** If OBS feels like a lot of setup, try LIVE Studio first. TikTok's [install guide](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Before-you-go-LIVE/Install-and-start-LIVE-Studio?lang=en) says it can import some settings from another streaming tool.

## Before you go LIVE with a stream key

A few lines on TikTok's pages save most failed desktop LIVEs.

- **Generate it close to start time** TikTok's Creator Academy says broadcast keys expire after a period of inactivity, so generate them no more than one hour in advance.
- **One device on the account** TikTok says using several devices on the account that's live can interfere, and its desktop steps say the LIVE can be interrupted and end if the profile is opened on other devices.
- **Bitrate** TikTok's in-app connecting page recommends 1.8 Mbps and says a bitrate that's too high may be transcoded automatically.
- **End it in both places** End the broadcast in your streaming software and on TikTok, and end the LIVE before you close the browser tab.
{: .cards}

If OBS can't connect, copy a fresh key and server URL from a new LIVE setup before you change anything else. An expired key is the problem TikTok itself warns about.
