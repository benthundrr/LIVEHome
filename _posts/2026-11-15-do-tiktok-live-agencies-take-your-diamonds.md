## How the money flows on TikTok LIVE

TikTok's [Virtual Items Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/virtual-items/en) lays out the whole chain. Viewers buy Coins, exchange them for Gifts, and send those Gifts during your LIVE.

1. **Viewers buy Coins** The price shows at the point of purchase. Coins can only be used to get Gifts, and can't be exchanged for cash.
2. **Coins become Gifts** The exchange rate between Coins and Gifts is displayed on TikTok when viewers make the exchange.
3. **Gifts become Diamonds** When a viewer sends a Gift to your LIVE, TikTok converts it into Diamonds in your account, at a conversion rate TikTok sets.
4. **Diamonds become rewards** You can choose to withdraw Diamonds for monetary compensation in US dollars, which TikTok calculates from factors including how many Diamonds you've collected.
{: .steps}

TikTok calls Diamonds "a measurement of the popularity" of your content. Nobody can buy Diamonds, and the policy says they can't be assigned or transferred to another user or third party unless TikTok expressly permits it in writing.

TikTok's [guide to LIVE Gifts](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-gifts-explained) adds who can collect Diamonds. You need to be at least 18 (19 in South Korea), live where LIVE Gifts are available, be eligible to go LIVE, and keep your account in good standing.

> **Tip** TikTok sets the Gift-to-Diamond conversion and calculates your payout. Be wary of anyone who quotes you a fixed rate that TikTok itself doesn't publish.
{: .callout .tip}

## Where the agency fits

TikTok describes [Creator Networks](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide), often called TikTok LIVE agencies, as third parties independent from TikTok. They support creators through livestream operations, educational resources and development guidance.

- **Creator development** Onboarding support, education and guidance to improve LIVE quality and consistency.
- **LIVE operations** Planning, scheduling and moderation guidance for your LIVEs.
- **Performance insights** Data that helps you understand how your audience engages.
- **Policy guidance** Help understanding TikTok's rules and keeping your account in good standing.
{: .cards}

### How agencies get paid

TikTok's [Creator Networks page](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en) says registered networks can take part in exclusive incentive programs for monthly bonuses. It also says [LIVE Backstage](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/what-is-tiktok-live-backstage), TikTok's operations platform for networks, is where they review those monthly bonuses and payouts.

So the agency money TikTok describes comes from TikTok's own programs. None of these pages describe an agency taking Diamonds out of your account.

An agency's own agreement is a separate matter. Any fee or share an agency charges you is set by its contract, so read the terms before you sign.

## Questions to ask any agency

TikTok notes that Creator Networks are independent businesses, and that it doesn't endorse, sponsor or take responsibility for their business activities. The agreement is between you and the agency, so ask these before you join.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Do you charge a fee to join? | Any joining fee is set by the agency, so ask up front. |
| Do you take any share of my Diamonds or rewards? | Your Diamonds stay in your account under TikTok's policy, so any share is an agency term. |
| Is there a contract, and how do I leave? | You should know the length and the exit before you sign. |
| How does the agency get paid? | TikTok describes monthly bonuses from its own incentive programs. |
| Who is my manager, and how do I reach them? | You should know who you will actually talk to, and where. |

> Your Diamonds are yours. Ask any agency to show you, in writing, how it gets paid.
