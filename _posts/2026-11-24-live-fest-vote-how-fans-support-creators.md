## The short answer

Fans support a creator in LIVE Fest 2026 in two ways: Votes and Gifts. Votes come from missions in the TikTok app. Gifts come from Coins. Both turn into points for the creator, and TikTok ranks creators on those points.

- **Votes** Collected from in-app missions and sent by hand. 1 Vote = 1 point, per TikTok's Event Rules
- **Gifts** Regular Gifts are 1 Coin = 1 point in TikTok's Power League video
- **FEST Gifts and Vault Gifts** 1 Coin = 2 points in the same video
- **Timing** Bonus Hour and Double Blast double the points while they run
{: .cards}

Every rate here comes from TikTok's in-app Event Rules and league videos. Confirm on your campaign page, because TikTok can change them. If you're a creator, this page is written so you can share it with your viewers.

## How fans collect Votes

TikTok gives viewers their own missions on the LIVE Fest campaign page. Finishing a mission earns Votes. TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) points fans to TikTok LIVE and [tiktok.com/livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) to find out how to support their favorite creators.

During the main competition, TikTok's Event Rules name these viewer missions:

- **Daily visit** Opening the campaign page each day
- **Gifting** Sending any Gift, and a daily Coin recharge
- **Posting** Posting a video with TikTok's LIVE Fest templates
- **Guardian** Becoming a creator's Guardian
- **Treasure Boxes** Votes that can appear in Treasure Boxes during a LIVE
{: .cards}

The number of Votes each mission gives is printed on the campaign page. We don't list figures here, because the page in your app is the one that counts.

> **Heads up** If a video that earned you Votes is removed or set to private before the campaign ends, TikTok's rules say those Votes are deducted. Keep your LIVE Fest videos public.
{: .callout .warn}

## How to send your Votes

Collecting Votes isn't enough. TikTok's Event Rules say Votes must be sent manually during Power League, Fandom League, Talent League and the global finals. A Vote sitting in your balance adds nothing to anyone's score.

1. **Update the app** The rules say an older version of TikTok may miss features or may not collect points at all.
2. **Open the campaign page** Find LIVE Fest in the TikTok app and check the viewer missions for today.
3. **Do the missions** Finish the ones you want and watch your Vote balance go up.
4. **Send to your creator** Send your Votes to the creator you're supporting. Each Vote you send is 1 point for them. Confirm on your campaign page.
5. **Don't sit on them** Unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, at the time shown on the campaign page.
{: .steps}

Votes can't be moved between accounts, so collect them on the account you'll use to send them.

> A Gift and a Vote are both support. Only the Vote you actually send becomes a point.
{: .pull}

## How Gifts, FEST Gifts and Vault Gifts count

Gifts are the other half of fan support. TikTok's in-app league videos give each type of Gift a point rate. Confirm on your campaign page.

| How you support | Points in TikTok's Power League video |
|---|---|
| Regular Gifts | 1 Coin = 1 point |
| FEST Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Vault Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Votes | 1 Vote = 1 point |

The leagues score a little differently, so the same Gift can count differently depending on when you send it. Confirm every rate on your campaign page.

- **Global finals** The rules add that Vault Gifts sent in combo are 1 Coin = 4 points
- **Fandom League** One viewer's daily contribution from FEST Gifts or regular Gifts is capped at 300,000 points, so many fans can count for more than one big gifter
- **Talent League** For Music and Dancing, TikTok's Talent video counts 1 gifter = 100 points and 100 viewers = 40 points, so showing up and sending one Gift matters
{: .cards}

You don't have to spend to help. Votes from missions that need no Coins still add points. How the whole score fits together is in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/).

## When your support counts most

Points can be worth double during two windows. Both are covered in [Double Blast and Bonus Hour in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-2026-double-blast-bonus-hour/).

- **Bonus Hour** A daily window of 2x points on Gifts and Votes, according to TikTok's Power League materials. Confirm the local time in the app.
- **Double Blast** A 30-minute 2x window the creator unlocks during their LIVE, once a day. It ends if the LIVE ends, and it can't be used during Bonus Hour.
{: .cards}

If your creator announces Double Blast or Bonus Hour, that's the time to send. Points sent before or after the window don't get the 2x. Confirm on your campaign page.

The calendar matters too. TikTok's newsroom says the campaign began November 9, and TikTok Japan's [announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. TikTok's LIVE Fest page lists these stages:

## Rules fans should know

The Event Rules cover viewers as well as creators. Breaking them can cost the creator you're trying to help. The full list is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/).

- **Age and region** The eligibility list starts at 18, or 19 in Korea, and you have to be in a listed region. Check the campaign page before you take part.
- **No fake engagement** Bots, agents, third-party plugins or any method used to fake engagement or collect points can get access blocked.
- **One account** Taking part with more than one account is against the rules.
- **No VPN** A VPN or other tool that hides your location fails the eligibility rules.
- **Your supporter region** The rules set it from the device location of your first Gift after the regional competition begins. It stays fixed for the rest of LIVE Fest 2026.
- **Opting out** A viewer who opts out in the campaign page settings disappears from supporter lists. Rejoining restores the earlier record.

> **Heads up** Nobody outside TikTok can sell you Votes or a shortcut to the top of a board. TikTok runs the leaderboards, and fake engagement can get a creator removed.
