## The short answer

Power League is the first league of TikTok LIVE Fest 2026, and it ends on December 6. TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) dates it from November 20 to December 6, 2026, then lists Fandom League from December 9 to 15 and the global finals from December 17 to 21.

When Power League closes, four things happen, based on what TikTok has published. Confirm each one on your campaign page in the TikTok app.

- **Totals are confirmed** TikTok's Event Rules say board points can contain errors, and TikTok confirms final totals after each module ends
- **Top creators advance** Top creators from the Legend, Crown and Rising tracks move on to the global finals
- **Rewards arrive** Virtual rewards go to your account after the module ends, and physical rewards come with claim instructions
- **The next leagues open** Fandom League and Talent League sit in the December 9 to 15 block
{: .cards}

The campaign itself keeps going. TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says LIVE Fest 2026 began November 9, while TikTok Japan's November 13 [press announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. Both say in-app competitions run through the end of December.

## Final totals and the path to the global finals

Don't treat the number on the board at the end of December 6 as final. TikTok's Event Rules say the points shown on a leaderboard may contain errors, and TikTok confirms the final totals after each module ends. Confirm on your campaign page.

TikTok's Power League one-pager in the app describes three rounds. The day-by-day dates for each round are on your campaign page.

1. **Round 1** You compete in the track TikTok placed you in. How many advance depends on the track.
2. **Round 2** Creators who advance merge into three tracks: Legend, Crown and Rising.
3. **Round 3** The same three tracks continue, and top creators from them advance to the global finals.
{: .steps}

TikTok's LIVE Fest page calls the global finals the ultimate stage where creators compete to be crowned Creator of 2026. TikTok's Event Rules list Power: Legend, Power: Crown and Power: Rising among the global track names. How the rounds work is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) guide.

> **Heads up** TikTok hasn't published how many creators advance from round 3 to the global finals. A post that gives one cutoff for every track is guessing.
{: .callout .warn}

## Your Power League rewards

TikTok's Event Rules say top creators on the points boards can claim rewards and Diamonds, and that the rewards list for each module isn't complete. Here's what the rules say happens once a module ends. Confirm on your campaign page.

| Reward type | What TikTok's Event Rules say |
|---|---|
| Virtual rewards | FEST effects, badges, profile frames and in-app announcements go to your TikTok account after the module ends |
| Profile frames | If you earn more than one, you choose which one to show |
| Physical rewards | Swag boxes and trophies are claimed through in-app instructions, which can ask for a mailing address |
| Swag levels | If you qualify for more than one level, you receive the highest level only |
| Claim deadlines | If you don't follow the claim instructions on time, you lose that reward |

Watch your TikTok inbox and campaign page in the days after December 6. A missed claim deadline is the easiest way to lose a reward you already earned. What TikTok has said about every LIVE Fest reward is in [LIVE Fest 2026 prizes and rewards](/blog/live-fest-2026-prizes-rewards/).

> **Tip** Nobody outside TikTok can hand you a LIVE Fest reward. Claim instructions arrive through the TikTok app, never through someone asking for a fee.
{: .callout .tip}

## What carries forward and what doesn't

Some things from Power League stay with you for the rest of LIVE Fest. Others don't, or TikTok hasn't said. Confirm each one on your campaign page.

- **Unused Votes** TikTok's rules say Votes have to be sent manually during Power League, Fandom League, Talent League and the global finals, and unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, at the time shown on the page. 1 Vote = 1 point.
- **Your LIVE Fest videos** TikTok's rules say Votes earned by a video are deducted if that video is removed or set to private before the campaign ends, so keep those videos public after December 6.
- **Your place on the boards** TikTok's rules say creators who opt out lose their points and rankings from the boards until they rejoin.
- **Power League points** TikTok's 5% carry-over rule, capped by track, is for moving between rounds. TikTok hasn't confirmed that any Power League points carry into Fandom League or Talent League.
{: .cards}

Eligibility doesn't end with Power League either. TikTok's rules say a VPN, a second account, bots or fake engagement can get you removed, so the same rules apply through the global finals. Why points go missing is covered in [LIVE Fest points not counting?](/blog/live-fest-points-not-counting/), and how fans send Votes is in [how fans vote and support a creator in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-vote-how-fans-support-creators/).

> Power League ending isn't the end of your LIVE Fest. Your viewers' unsent Votes and your next league are still ahead.
{: .pull}

## December 7 and 8, then Fandom and Talent League

TikTok's public LIVE Fest page doesn't list any league on December 7 or 8. Power League ends December 6 and Fandom League starts December 9. TikTok hasn't said what, if anything, runs in between, so check your campaign page before you plan those two days.

Fandom League runs December 9 to 15, and TikTok's page lists Talent League in the same block, with no date line of its own. Each league places you differently.

| League | How TikTok places you | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fandom League | One of six tracks, from your number of active fans as of a date on the campaign page | TikTok's Fandom video and Event Rules |
| Talent League | Music, Dancing or Gaming, with an audition line for each track; the audition started November 6 | TikTok's Talent video and Event Rules |
| Global finals | Top creators from Power League, Fandom League and Talent League compete on global boards | TikTok's Event Rules |

TikTok's rules say one creator can be listed on up to three global finals boards, one track from each league. So a creator who doesn't advance from Power League can still aim for a Fandom or Talent board at the finals. Confirm on your campaign page.

1. **Read your Fandom track** Check the track TikTok assigned you and the active-fan date it used. The [Fandom League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/) has a checklist.
2. **Check your Talent League track** If you entered Music, Dancing or Gaming, TikTok's rules say your LIVEs have to keep showing that talent. See the [Talent League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/).
3. **Remind viewers about Votes** TikTok's rules let viewers send Votes during Fandom League, Talent League and the global finals, and unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals.
4. **Confirm your rates** Each league scores differently. Confirm every points rate on your campaign page.
{: .steps}

Crossover League isn't on TikTok's public schedule, so check your campaign page for its dates. It's covered in the [Crossover League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-crossover-league/).

## What TikTok hasn't confirmed

Some things creators ask about after Power League aren't public. TikTok shows them in the app, or hasn't published them at all.

- **Round 3 advance counts** TikTok hasn't published how many creators move from each Power League track to the global finals.
- **December 7 and 8** TikTok's public page lists no league on those days and hasn't said what happens then.
- **Points between leagues** TikTok hasn't confirmed whether any Power League points carry into Fandom League or Talent League.
- **Exact Talent League dates** TikTok's public page gives Talent League no date line of its own.
- **Reward delivery dates** TikTok's rules say virtual rewards arrive after the module ends, without giving a date.
- **The Tokyo gala** TikTok says the global gala is in Tokyo in 2027, after regional events from January 2027. It hasn't announced a date or venue.
