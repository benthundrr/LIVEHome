## Why viewers send Gifts

TikTok says viewers send [LIVE Gifts](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-gifts-explained) "to show appreciation and support for creators' content in real time." It points to entertaining performances, interactive moments and community engagement as the times Gifts tend to arrive.

Viewers use Coins to send Gifts, and Gifts generate Diamonds for you. TikTok's [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) says the number of Diamonds is one of several factors behind your LIVE rewards.

So the fastest route to more Gifts runs through a better LIVE. Viewers who know what's happening, feel seen and have a way to take part are the ones who gift.

- **A show worth watching** A clear topic, a performance or a game that's happening right now.
- **An easy way in** A named Gift and what it does in your LIVE.
- **A real response** A thank-you by name, a shout-out or a choice that changes what happens next.
{: .cards}

## LIVE goals and small Gift prompts

TikTok's [LIVE goal](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Set-a-LIVE-goal-to-welcome-more-Gifts) shows viewers a target for specific Gifts during your LIVE. TikTok says it gives viewers "a clear call to action."

1. **Open LIVE Goal** Open the LIVE Goal pop-up.
2. **Describe the goal** Explain how you'll celebrate once it's reached.
3. **Pick the Gifts** Choose the Gifts and the quantity you want.
4. **Add a prize and show it** Edit an optional prize for contributors and turn on the sticker.
{: .steps}

TikTok's advice is to set a goal that's realistic. A goal that's too high can seem out of reach, and one that's too easy might not inspire anyone.

### Name a small Gift and what it does

TikTok's [small Gift guidance](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Small-Gift-Guidance-How-to-Encourage-Low-Cost-Gifts-in-Your-LIVE) covers prompting viewers toward a low-price Gift such as a Rose or GG. Its examples tie the Gift to something in the LIVE, like "Drop a GG and you get to pick my next character."

Keep it short and pair your voice with on-screen text, so late arrivals can follow too. TikTok says short lines usually work better on LIVE.

> **Tip** Tell viewers what to send, and what happens when they do. That's an invitation to play along.
{: .callout .tip}

## Thank gifters the right way

TikTok's [Gift thanking guide](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Gift-Thanking-How-to-Acknowledge-Viewer-Gifts-Naturally-in-Your-LIVE) says to thank viewers soon after they send a Gift, and to connect the thanks to the Gift. A line like "Mia, thanks for the Rocket!" feels more personal than a plain thank-you.

| Moment | Line TikTok suggests |
|---|---|
| The name is clear | "Thank you, Alex!" |
| You want to respond fast | "Thanks for the Rose!" |
| Gifts arrive quickly | "Thank you guys for all the Gifts." |
| A viewer joins your Fan Club | "Thanks for joining the Fan Club!" |

TikTok also notes what doesn't count as Gift thanking: thanks for a follow or a share, a plain welcome, or thanks for support on another platform.

## Polls, Gift votes and LIVE Battles

[Poll and Gift vote](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Let-each-viewer-shape-what-happens-next) in LIVE Studio lets viewers pick what happens next, like the next game or topic. With Vote with Gifts, viewers vote by sending one of the displayed Gifts.

Find it under Tools, Engage, Poll and Gift vote. Only one poll or Gift vote can run at a time.

### LIVE Battles

A [LIVE match](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Start-a-LIVE-match-to-call-on-your-supporters), which creators call a LIVE Battle, puts you head to head with another creator in a co-host LIVE. TikTok's LIVE Studio help says each Coin of a Gift counts as one match point, and the creator or team with the most points wins.

Battles give your viewers a side to be on. Our guide on [how to win TikTok LIVE Battles](/blog/how-to-win-tiktok-live-battles/) covers points, Power-ups and timing.

## What gets Gifts restricted

TikTok's [LIVE Monetization Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/rules_and_guidance/live_monetization_guidelines) list gift baiting as ineligible for monetization. TikTok may temporarily restrict monetization features, and repeated or severe violations can lead to permanent restrictions.

- **Baiting** Tricking viewers, or mechanically and repeatedly soliciting Gifts through deceptive or non-interactive behavior.
- **Exchanges** Follow-for-follow promises, or offering likes, follows or Gifts in exchange for engagement.
- **Pressure** Using viewers' emotions, sympathy or sense of obligation to push them to gift.
- **Money asks** Asking for Gifts or financial aid based on personal financial need or crowdfunding unrelated to your content.
{: .cards}

The guidelines also restrict LIVEs where a creator only presents two opposing viewpoints and asks viewers to send Gifts. You're responsible for guests too, since TikTok holds creators accountable for content from everyone in a multi-guest LIVE.

> **Heads up** A LIVE that misleads viewers or manipulates engagement can lose access to Gifts. Keep every Gift prompt honest and tied to your content.
{: .callout .warn}
