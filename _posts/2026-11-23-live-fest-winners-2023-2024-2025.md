## The short answer

TikTok has named LIVE Fest winners for one year so far: LIVE Fest 2025. Its newsroom recap of the Las Vegas event on February 12, 2026 lists the Top 1 creator in seven categories.

For LIVE Fest 2023 and LIVE Fest 2024, TikTok's newsroom gives participation numbers, hosts and cities, but no list of winners. Any full 2023 or 2024 winners list you find elsewhere doesn't come from TikTok's newsroom.

| Campaign | Where winners were celebrated | Winners named by TikTok's newsroom |
|---|---|---|
| LIVE Fest 2023 | Miami | None |
| LIVE Fest 2024 | London, January 16, 2025 | None |
| LIVE Fest 2025 | Las Vegas, February 12, 2026 | Seven category winners, plus Take the Stage |
| LIVE Fest 2026 | Tokyo, 2027 | Not yet |

### Take the Stage and the US regional event

During the main show, three creators joined remotely for a singing competition on LIVE called Take the Stage. TikTok says @thecyrilia was crowned overall champion.

The week started with Regional LIVE Fest for US creators on February 9 at Area 15 in Las Vegas. TikTok says DJ Steve Aoki (@steveaoki) received the Entertainment LIVE of the Year Award there.

## LIVE Fest 2024 results

TikTok's [2024 LIVE review](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/celebrating-the-power-of-community-and-creativity-on-tiktok-live-in-2024) says LIVE Fest 2024 kicked off in October 2024 with regional and global competitions spanning multiple months. Almost 6M creators took part, and 477M viewers tuned into the event page.

The campaign ended with a global celebration in London on January 16, 2025, with creators from over 60 countries. Jason Derulo hosted, and Zara Larsson headlined.

The review congratulates the top LIVE creators who celebrated in London, but it doesn't name LIVE Fest winners or categories. It does spotlight four LIVE creators, @captaineriklive, @puzzlingbeats, @fiaahamilton and @strictlypipa, as part of its look back at the year. TikTok doesn't present them as LIVE Fest winners.

## LIVE Fest 2023 results

LIVE Fest started in 2023. TikTok's [launch post](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/live-fest-2023), dated November 13, 2023, called it a global event across 50+ countries and regions, open to all eligible creators.

TikTok's [LIVE Fest 2023 recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/live-fest-2023-recap), dated February 7, 2024, calls it the first-ever LIVE Fest. It says the campaign ran from November to January, with 2.3M creators taking part and 38M people tuning into the event page.

The recap says LIVE Fest 2023 wrapped with a Miami celebration in February, hosted by Terry Crews, with a performance by Meghan Trainor. It congratulates the top LIVE creators who traveled from around the world to attend, without naming them.

TikTok also teamed up with the United Nations World Food Programme during LIVE Fest 2023, aiming to raise $500k to fight hunger. TikTok says over 1M users helped unlock its donation.

## How the format changed, 2023 to 2026

Each year, LIVE Fest has kept the same arc: an in-app competition first, then a global celebration for top creators. What changed is how creators compete.

### 2023: one points ranking

TikTok's 2023 launch post says creators took part by going LIVE during the campaign, with special LIVE Fest challenges and community rankings on top. Creators were ranked automatically on the points they collected, and top-ranked creators could receive rewards. Winners could also be invited to a global creator award event early the next year and receive extra Diamond rewards.

### 2024: regional and global competitions

TikTok describes LIVE Fest 2024 as regional and global competitions spanning multiple months, starting in October 2024. Its newsroom doesn't describe the 2024 tracks. The 2024 award titles printed for Thundrr use the name Diamond Track, in both LIVE FEST 2024 and Community FEST 2024, a separate TikTok LIVE event.

### 2025: four leagues, seven categories

In TikTok's newsroom recaps, LIVE Fest 2025 is the first year with named leagues: Power League, Fandom League, Talent League and Crossover League. Power League had a Rising Track, and Talent League split into Gaming, Dancing and Music tracks, for seven winner categories in all.

### 2026: the same leagues, a new stage

LIVE Fest 2026 keeps the four league names. TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement says the campaign began November 9, and TikTok Japan says the in-app ranking opened November 6. The global gala moves to Tokyo in 2027.

- **Power League** Gifts-led rankings. See the [Power League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/).
- **Fandom League** Fan support. See the [Fandom League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/).
- **Talent League** Music, dancing and gaming. See the [Talent League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/).
- **Crossover League** Short videos plus LIVE. See the [Crossover League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-crossover-league/).
