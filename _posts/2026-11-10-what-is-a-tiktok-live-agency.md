## What a Creator Network is, in TikTok's terms

A TikTok LIVE agency, which TikTok calls a LIVE Creator Network, is an independent company that helps TikTok LIVE creators grow. It coaches creators on streaming, scheduling, LIVE Battles and audience engagement, and helps them understand TikTok's LIVE policies.

In its [guide to Creator Networks](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide), TikTok describes them as "third parties independent from TikTok dedicated to supporting creators through livestream operations, educational resources, and development guidance." It adds that they are "sometimes referred to as TikTok LIVE agencies."

- **Independent businesses** TikTok says networks operate on their own behalf, and it doesn't endorse, sponsor or take responsibility for them.
- **Registered with TikTok** A company applies through LIVE Backstage, which TikTok calls the official operation platform for Creator Networks, and TikTok reviews the application.
- **Joined in the app** Networks send invitations through TikTok's own system, and you accept them in the TikTok app.

## What an agency does day to day

TikTok lists the kinds of support a network may offer: onboarding, help planning and scheduling LIVEs, moderation guidance, performance insights, community support, and policy guidance. Its [comparison of networks and solo creators](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-network-vs-individual-creator) also mentions creative training and account safety support.

## How agencies get paid

On TikTok LIVE, viewers use Coins to send virtual Gifts, and Gifts generate Diamonds for the creator. TikTok's [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) says the number of Diamonds is one of several factors behind a creator's LIVE rewards under its Reward Policy. TikTok sets those rewards.

Business Insider reported in 2023 that TikTok paid agencies a cut of its own portion of the net revenue from their creators' gifts. In the same report, a TikTok spokesperson said the program was free for creators to join and that creators kept their full share of gift revenue.

### A network usually makes sense if

You already go LIVE, or you're ready to, and you want to stream regularly. It also helps if you're getting gifts but can't tell what's working, or you want to get better at Battles and rankings.

### It may not be for you yet if

You only go LIVE once in a while, because the support pays off only when you show up. To collect Diamonds, TikTok requires you to be at least 18 (19 in South Korea), to live where LIVE Gifts are available, to be eligible to go LIVE, and to have an account in good standing.

> **Tip** No legitimate network can promise you viewers or income. Treat any promise like that as a warning sign.
{: .callout .tip}

## How to check an agency is real

Scam invitations exist, so check before you accept anything. Our full checklist is in [Are TikTok LIVE agencies legit?](/blog/are-tiktok-live-agencies-legit/)

1. **Use the in-app flow** TikTok's [agency invite page](https://www.tiktok.com/tcn/agency_invite) puts your invitation code under Settings and privacy, Creator tools, LIVE Center, Agency Center.
2. **Check the account** Make sure the name on the invitation matches the network you've been talking to. Thundrr's official account is @thundrr.co.
3. **Never pay or share your login** TikTok says it will never ask for your password, and a real network doesn't need it either.
4. **Get the terms in writing** Ask about any fees, any minimum hours, and how to leave before you accept.
{: .steps}
