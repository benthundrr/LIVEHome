## What changed

On Monday, November 23, 2026, TikTok LIVE and Food Network hosted TikTok LIVE x Food Network Present: Giving Season, a three-hour Thanksgiving cooking livestream. It was hosted by @mattpeterson_ from the Food Network Kitchen, with @bobby_flay_, @chefbrookew, @rashadjonesbbq, @emmanuelduverneau and @guarnaschelli cooking.

According to the [TikTok Newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/giving-season-comes-alive-on-tiktok-live-with-food-network?lang=en), it became the most-watched food livestream on TikTok LIVE in the US to date, with 961,000 viewers. Viewers voted in nine interactive polls and asked the chefs questions in the chat.

The part that matters for every LIVE creator: viewers sent special food-themed gifts, and each one triggered a donation from TikTok to No Kid Hungry. TikTok says those special gifts can keep being sent in any TikTok LIVE room, including yours.

- **961,000** viewers, per TikTok
- **9** interactive polls in the LIVE
- **$100,000** TikTok's goal for No Kid Hungry

## Who it affects

- **LIVE creators** The special gifts can show up in your LIVE room, whatever you stream.
- **Your viewers** Anyone who gifts can choose one of the special gifts and TikTok makes the donation.
- **Food and holiday streams** Cooking, baking and family LIVEs fit the theme naturally.
{: .cards}

## When it runs

TikTok says viewers can keep sending the special gifts through December 25, 2026, or until TikTok reaches its goal of $100,000 for No Kid Hungry. If the goal is reached first, the gifts may end early.

TikTok and Food Network are also rebroadcasting the livestream on Wednesday, November 25 at 2:00pm ET on @foodnetwork, @tiktoklive_us and other accounts. TikTok also teased the next Flavors Come Alive livestream from Food Network and TikTok LIVE.

> **What TikTok hasn't said** The announcement doesn't name the gifts, list their coin prices or say how Diamonds work for them. Open the gift panel in the app to see which gifts are marked and what they cost.

## What LIVE creators should do now

1. **Find the gifts** Check your gift panel before you go LIVE so you know what the special gifts look like.
2. **Mention it once, plainly** Something like: TikTok is donating to No Kid Hungry for each of its special food gifts through December 25. Say it, then get back to your stream.
3. **Don't pressure anyone** Never guilt viewers into gifting or tie the cause to your goals. Thank every supporter the same way, whatever they send.
4. **Don't promise your own donation** The donation comes from TikTok. Don't say you'll match it or give a share unless you really will and can show it.
5. **Follow the LIVE rules** TikTok's community guidelines and LIVE gift rules still apply. This isn't a giveaway, so don't promise prizes for gifts.
