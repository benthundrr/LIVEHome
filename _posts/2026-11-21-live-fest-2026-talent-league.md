## What Talent League is

Talent League is the LIVE Fest 2026 league where, in TikTok's words, creators showcase their unique talents. It has three tracks, Music, Dancing and Gaming, and runs across two rounds.

Talent League sits inside a longer campaign. TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, while TikTok's Japan announcement says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. TikTok's Event Rules say the Talent League audition also started November 6.

- **Tracks** Music, Dancing and Gaming
- **Rounds** Two, with the day-by-day calendar in the app
- **Audition** Started November 6, per TikTok's Event Rules
- **What comes next** Global finals, December 17 to 21
{: .cards}

TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) lists Talent League beside Fandom League, December 9 to 15, but leaves the Talent League date line empty. TikTok hasn't published exact Talent League dates on that page, so treat your campaign page as the source. Every other date is in the [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## Audition requirements for each track

TikTok's Talent video sets one audition line per track. These are the lines as TikTok states them. Your campaign page shows whether you meet them.

| Track | Audition line in TikTok's Talent video |
|---|---|
| Music | Music-certified creators |
| Dancing | 3 dance LIVEs in the past 7 days, with at least 5% dance content |
| Gaming | At least 50% gaming content across 14 days |

Creators who aren't pre-assigned to a track can select one on the campaign page. TikTok hasn't published how a creator becomes music-certified, so check your page if you plan to enter Music.

> **Tip** Look at the last two weeks of your LIVEs before you pick a track. The Dancing line counts the past 7 days and the Gaming line counts 14 days, so your recent LIVEs are what TikTok checks.
{: .callout .tip}

## Staying on your track

Choosing a track is a commitment. TikTok's rules say that once your track is confirmed, your LIVE has to keep showing that talent, or you may be removed from Talent League.

Each track also has a content standard in the Event Rules. Missing it can revoke your eligibility for the league.

- **Music** Your LIVE has to include music performance or audience interaction, and you need the rights to the music you use.
- **Dancing** Dancing has to stay the core of the LIVE.
- **Gaming** At least 50% of the LIVE has to feature gaming-related content.
{: .cards}

## How Talent League scores a LIVE

TikTok's Talent video shows these base rates. Rates can change during the campaign, so confirm each one on your campaign page.

| What counts | Music and Dancing | Gaming |
|---|---|---|
| FEST Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points, including interactive Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Regular Gifts | Not listed for these tracks | 1 Coin = 1 point |
| Votes | 1 Vote = 1 point | 1 Vote = 1 point |
| Gifters | 1 gifter = 100 points | 1 gifter = 100 points |
| Viewers | 100 viewers = 40 points | Not listed for this track |
| Super Fans | Not listed for these tracks | 1 Super Fan = 500 points |

Those gifter, viewer and Super Fan lines mean Talent League counts how many people support you, as well as how much they send. Confirm every rate on your campaign page.

Votes only count when viewers send them. TikTok's rules say Votes have to be sent manually during Talent League, at 1 Vote = 1 point. Confirm on your campaign page.

The full scoring comparison across leagues is in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/).

## Take the Stage

Take the Stage is a Music and Dancing bonus. TikTok's rules say the highest-scoring Take the Stage of the day counts as 5x points. Confirm on your campaign page.

1. **Try as often as you like** The rules say Take the Stage attempts are unlimited, and only the day's best one gets the 5x bonus.
2. **Watch the clock** A session that crosses midnight counts on the day it ends.
3. **Bring your regular viewers** Plan your strongest performance for a time when the people who send Gifts and Votes are on.
{: .steps}

The name has history. During the main show at LIVE Fest 2025 in Las Vegas, TikTok's [recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) describes a Take the Stage moment where three creators joined remotely for a singing competition on LIVE, and @thecyrilia was crowned the overall champion.

> Pick the track you already go LIVE with, then make one Take the Stage a day count.
{: .pull}

## Global finals, past winners and what's unconfirmed

TikTok's LIVE Fest page dates the global finals from December 17 to 21, 2026. TikTok's rules say top creators from Power League, Fandom League and Talent League compete there on global boards, with Music, Dancing and Gaming among the global track names. One creator can be listed on up to three boards, one track from each league.

TikTok's Las Vegas recap names the LIVE Fest 2025 Talent League winners, the top creator in each track:

| Track | LIVE Fest 2025 winner |
|---|---|
| Talent League Gaming Track | @vx44000 |
| Talent League Dancing Track | @kenkenpa.com |
| Talent League Music Track | @mamila_0602 |

Some Talent League details aren't public. TikTok shows them in the app, or hasn't published them at all.

- **Exact dates** TikTok's public page gives Talent League no date line of its own, so the start and end dates are unconfirmed.
- **Round calendar** Talent League has two rounds, and the day-by-day calendar is in the app only.
- **Advance counts** TikTok hasn't published how many creators move from round 1 to round 2.
- **Music certification** TikTok hasn't published how a creator becomes music-certified.
