## What Crossover League is

Crossover League is the LIVE Fest 2026 league built for short-video creators who also go LIVE. It sits beside Power League, Fandom League and Talent League.

TikTok's [@tiktok_livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiktok_livefest) account sums it up in one line: post short videos, go LIVE and grow your community to collect points and level up. Your short videos and your LIVEs both count toward the same Crossover score.

- **Who it's for** Short-video creators who also go LIVE
- **How you score** Posting short videos, going LIVE and growing your community
- **How you progress** Levels, with Level 3 unlocking a global board
- **Top prize named by TikTok** A Top 10 spot and the Crossover Icon title
{: .cards}

Crossover League runs inside the wider LIVE Fest 2026 campaign. TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, while TikTok's Japan announcement says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. Both say in-app competitions run through the end of December.

## How Crossover points add up

TikTok's Crossover video counts posts, LIVEs, followers, likes, Gifts and Fan Club members toward points and level rewards. Those fall into three groups.

- **Short videos** The views and likes on the videos you post
- **LIVE** Going LIVE, plus the Gifts your viewers send
- **Community** New followers and new Fan Club members
{: .cards}

TikTok prints the rate for each of these on the campaign page, and the rates can change during the campaign. Confirm every points rate on your campaign page before you plan around it.

> **Heads up** If a post gives you exact Crossover point rates or level cutoffs, check them against your campaign page. Your page is the only number that counts for your account.
{: .callout .warn}

TikTok's rules for LIVE Fest videos also matter here. Points or Votes earned from a campaign video can be taken back if that video is removed or set to private before the campaign ends, so keep your LIVE Fest videos public. Confirm on your campaign page.

How Crossover scoring sits next to the other leagues is in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/).

## Levels and the Crossover Icon board

Crossover League is built around levels. TikTok's Crossover post says you collect points to level up, and reaching Level 3 unlocks the Global Crossover Leaderboard.

1. **Collect points** Post short videos, go LIVE and grow your community.
2. **Level up** TikTok shows the points each level needs on your campaign page.
3. **Reach Level 3** That unlocks the Global Crossover Leaderboard.
4. **Chase the Top 10** Creators in the Top 10 of that board can become a Crossover Icon.
{: .steps}

The levels come with level rewards, according to TikTok's Crossover video. TikTok shows what each level unlocks, and the threshold for each one, on the campaign page. Confirm on your campaign page.

## A weekly plan for short-video creators

Crossover League rewards both halves of your account. These steps come straight from what TikTok says counts. They don't add any rule of their own.

- **Post on a rhythm** Short videos are half of the Crossover idea, so keep a posting schedule you can hold through December.
- **Point viewers to your LIVE** Use your videos to tell people when you go LIVE next.
- **Go LIVE on set days** LIVEs and the Gifts sent in them count, so pick days your viewers can count on.
- **Grow your community** New followers and new Fan Club members count, so invite viewers to follow and join your Fan Club.
- **Check your level every few days** Your campaign page shows how close you are to the next level.

> Your videos bring people in and your LIVEs keep them, and Crossover League counts both.
{: .pull}

## Last year's winner and what's unconfirmed

Crossover League ran in LIVE Fest 2025 too. TikTok's [Las Vegas recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) names @u_u2xn as the top creator in Crossover League, one of seven category winners honored at Planet Hollywood on February 12, 2026.

Some Crossover details aren't public. TikTok shows them in the app, or hasn't published them at all.

- **Dates** TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) lists Power League, Fandom League and the global finals, with no Crossover League date line.
- **Point rates and thresholds** These are on your campaign page in the app.
- **Level rewards** TikTok's video mentions level rewards, and the campaign page shows what each level unlocks.
- **Link to the global finals** The global finals run December 17 to 21 on TikTok's page. TikTok hasn't said how the Global Crossover Leaderboard fits with them.
