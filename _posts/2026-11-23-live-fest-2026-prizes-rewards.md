## The short answer

LIVE Fest 2026 rewards come from TikTok, and the details sit on your campaign page in the app. TikTok's public pages describe the honors. The in-app Event Rules describe the rewards and how you claim them.

- **The title** TikTok's LIVE Fest page says the global finals crown the Creator of 2026
- **In-app rewards** Effects, badges, profile frames and in-app announcements, per the Event Rules
- **Physical rewards** Swag and trophies, claimed through in-app instructions
- **The gala** A possible invitation to an offline event, with the global gala in Tokyo in 2027
{: .cards}

TikTok hasn't published any prize amounts. Confirm on your campaign page what applies to your account.

## What TikTok's public pages say

TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) tells creators to join the world's best creators and compete for the highest honors and red carpet fame. It describes the global finals, December 17 to 21, as the ultimate stage where creators compete to be crowned Creator of 2026.

TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, with in-app competitions through the end of December. TikTok Japan's November 13 [announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. Both say the campaign builds to a global gala in Tokyo in 2027.

Regional events come first. TikTok says they begin in January 2027 across Southeast Asia, Latin America, Europe and the United States.

TikTok also brought back the LIVE Fest Creator Academy in the United States, Vietnam, Indonesia and Latin America. It's a set of creator-led LIVE sessions to help you prepare, open in participating markets.

> **Tip** The newsroom and the LIVE Fest page describe honors, not amounts. If a post gives you a Diamond figure for a 2026 prize, ask where TikTok published it.
{: .callout .tip}

## What the in-app Event Rules describe

The Event Rules appear inside the TikTok app on your campaign page, not on TikTok's public web pages. Our [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/) guide summarizes them. Confirm on your campaign page, because TikTok can change the list.

### Rewards and Diamonds

The rules say top creators on the points boards can claim rewards and Diamonds, and TikTok treats the two separately. Rewards are whatever your module lists, and the rules say that list isn't complete.

### Virtual rewards

Virtual rewards include special FEST effects, badges, profile frames and in-app announcements. They go to your TikTok account after the module ends. If you earn more than one profile frame, you choose which one to show.

### Physical rewards

Swag boxes and trophies are claimed through in-app instructions, which can ask for a mailing address. If you qualify for more than one level of swag, you receive the highest level only.

### Claim deadlines

If you don't follow the claim instructions on time, you lose that reward. If TikTok can't confirm a winner, the reward can go to the next person under the same criteria.

## The Tokyo gala invitation and travel

The invitation people ask about is to an offline event, such as a regional event or the Tokyo gala. The in-app rules limit it. Confirm on your campaign page.

1. **One invitation per creator** Each creator can win only one invitation to an offline ceremony or event. If you win a second one, TikTok can give it to the next creator on the board.
2. **No guarantee** A top finish can make you eligible, but no creator is promised an invitation or a seat at the gala.
3. **Travel at your cost** Costs the invitation doesn't cover are yours, including a passport, a visa and anything else the invitation message leaves out.
4. **Conditions** TikTok can ask for a signed waiver, and it can refuse entry if it reasonably believes you broke the rules.
{: .steps}

TikTok Japan says details of the global ceremony will be announced later. There's no public date or venue for the Tokyo gala yet. What we know so far is in [LIVE Fest 2026 global finals and Tokyo gala](/blog/live-fest-2026-global-finals-tokyo/).

> **Heads up** Nobody outside TikTok can sell you a seat at the gala or guarantee a Tokyo invitation. A real invitation arrives through TikTok, never through someone asking for a fee.
{: .callout .warn}
