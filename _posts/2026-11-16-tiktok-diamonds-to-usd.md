## Coins vs Gifts vs Diamonds

Three virtual items sit between a viewer's wallet and your payout. TikTok's [Virtual Items Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/virtual-items/en) defines each one.

| Item | Who has it | What TikTok says about it |
|---|---|---|
| Coins | Viewers | Bought in the app or on the website. The price shows at the point of purchase, and Coins can't be exchanged for cash. |
| Gifts | Viewers, then sent to you | Activated with Coins. The number of Coins each Gift needs is displayed below the Gift. |
| Diamonds | Creators | Collected from the Gifts you receive. Nobody can buy Diamonds, and they can't be exchanged back for Coins or Gifts. |

Here's how one Gift moves through the chain.

1. **A viewer buys Coins** TikTok's [Gifts and Coins help page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/gifts/gifts) says Coins can be bought in the app through the app store, on the TikTok website in supported countries, or with TikTok gift cards where available.
2. **The viewer sends a Gift** During your LIVE, they tap the Gift button, pick a Gift and tap Send. The Gift's value in Coins comes out of their balance.
3. **TikTok converts it into Diamonds** The policy says a Gift sent to your LIVE is converted into Diamonds in your account, at a conversion rate TikTok determines from time to time.
4. **You withdraw for US dollars** You can choose to withdraw Diamonds for monetary compensation denominated in US dollars, calculated by TikTok.
{: .steps}

## What TikTok publishes, and what it doesn't

TikTok spells out the steps, the eligibility rules and where to find your balance. It doesn't publish a fixed Diamond-to-dollar rate anywhere on its policy or help pages.

### What TikTok does say

- **TikTok sets the rate** Diamonds are based on the Gifts you receive, at a conversion rate TikTok sets from time to time in its sole discretion.
- **Your payout is calculated** TikTok calculates the dollar amount from various factors, including how many Diamonds you've collected.
- **The rate is shown at withdrawal** The withdrawal rate is displayed at the point of withdrawal, along with any daily limits on withdrawal amounts.
- **No transfers** No Diamonds can be transferred to another user or a third party unless TikTok expressly permits it in writing.
{: .cards}

TikTok's [Diamonds help page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/video-gifts-on-tiktok/diamonds) describes Diamonds as a way to recognize creator popularity, ranking and contributions. It adds that creators can also collect Diamonds through missions and campaigns, and may be eligible for rewards such as digital goods or services.

> **Heads up** Any diamonds calculator multiplies by a rate somebody assumed. TikTok doesn't publish one, so treat any fixed number as a guess and go by the rate TikTok shows you when you withdraw.
{: .callout .warn}

### Who can collect Diamonds

TikTok's [guide to LIVE Gifts](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-gifts-explained) lists the requirements. You need to live where LIVE Gifts are available, be at least 18 (19 in South Korea), be eligible to go LIVE and keep your account in good standing.

Government, politician and political party accounts, plus certain public interest accounts, can't collect Diamonds through LIVE Gifts.

## Where to see your own balance

Your own balance is the only number that matters. The policy says you can see your Diamonds on a real-time basis by checking your account.

1. **Open your profile** In the TikTok app, tap Profile at the bottom.
2. **Go to Balance** Tap the Menu button at the top, then select Balance.
3. **Tap LIVE rewards** Your Diamonds balance is here. For questions about it, tap Help & feedback.
{: .steps}

That's also where the withdrawal options live. The policy says you withdraw by selecting the relevant options in your account, and the rate and any daily limits are displayed at the point of withdrawal.

## Why your payout can differ

Your dollar amount depends on more than your Diamond count. TikTok's policy points to several factors.

| Factor | What TikTok's policy says |
|---|---|
| The conversion rate | TikTok sets the Gift-to-Diamond rate and can change it from time to time. |
| How the payout is calculated | TikTok uses various factors, including the number of Diamonds you've collected. |
| Withdrawal terms | The rate and any daily limits are shown at the point of withdrawal. |
| Payment channel | Cash goes to your nominated PayPal account or another third-party payment channel, and that provider's terms and fees apply to any currency conversion. |
| Taxes | You're responsible for taxes on payments, and TikTok may deduct taxes where the law requires it. |
| Refunds | If a viewer is entitled to a refund for Gifts, the payment you withdrew for those Gifts is forfeited. |
| Account standing | TikTok can deduct Diamonds if you break the policy. |

TikTok also says it doesn't guarantee withdrawals within a specific time, and that all Coins, Gifts and Diamonds are cancelled if an account is closed. Withdraw before you ever close an account.

> Diamonds turn into dollars at the rate TikTok shows you at withdrawal, so that's the only rate to trust.
