## What changed

TikTok Shop US updated its [Group Deal guide](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4048337703995157&default_language=en) on its US Seller University on November 20, 2026.

Group Deal is a LIVE-only group-buy. You pick a product, set a group price and a group size, and shoppers join from your LIVE to unlock that price together. They pre-authorize payment when they join and are only charged if their group unlocks the deal.

The headline change is a second mode, **Tiered group**. Here's how it compares with the original Fixed group, according to TikTok's guide:

| | Fixed group | Tiered group |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | One group size, one price | Several tiers, each larger group size unlocks a lower price |
| Group size | 2 to 6 shoppers | 2 to 20 shoppers per tier, up to 20 tiers |
| Groups | A new group opens each time one fills | One shared group |
| Price shoppers pay | The Group Deal price | The price of the highest tier reached |
| Shoppers are charged | As soon as their group fills | When the activity ends |

TikTok's example: a $100 product with Tier 1 at 5 shoppers for $80 and Tier 2 at 10 shoppers for $60. With 4 shoppers, nobody is charged. With 5 to 9, everyone pays $80. If 10 join, the deal ends right away and everyone pays $60.

### Other additions

- **Virtual bundles** One group price can cover a set or stock-up pack.
- **LIVE-only products** A product or bundle can be made available only in your LIVE.
- **Unpin without ending** A running deal keeps going if you pin something else. Shoppers can still join from the shopping bag.
- **Several deals at once** You can run several Group Deals and Price Cuts at the same time, each on a different product, for up to 24 hours.
- **Coupon tags** The Group Deal card shows the best coupon a shopper can use.

## Who it affects

- **Creators who sell on LIVE** TikTok says the linked creator needs a Creator Health Score above 150.
- **Sellers and brand hosts** The seller needs a Shop Performance Score of 3.5 or higher.
- **Shoppers in your LIVE** They see the deal on the pinned card and in your shopping bag, with progress, time left and the next price.

## When it starts

The updated guide is dated November 20, 2026, and describes the new options as available now in LIVE Manager on PC and in the app. Check the Group Deal tab in your LIVE console to see whether your account is eligible.

> **Heads up** Stock isn't reserved when you create a deal. It's checked and locked when you press Start, and a deal can be blocked if stock runs short.

## What LIVE creators should do now

1. **Check eligibility** Confirm the Group Deal tab shows up in your LIVE console. If it doesn't, check your Creator Health Score and ask the seller about their Shop Performance Score.
2. **Pick the right product** Only products with one selling price across all SKUs work. Virtual bundles and LIVE-only products can be used too.
3. **Explain the rules out loud** TikTok says shoppers can't cancel after joining, so say how the deal works before you ask people to join.
4. **Keep Tier 1 easy** TikTok suggests using Tiered group in your busiest moments, with a first tier your viewers can reach and a top price planned around margin.
5. **Pin it yourself** Starting a deal doesn't pin the product. Pin it before you press Start to build interest.
{: .steps}

For a Fixed group, TikTok suggests starting with a 10% discount, a group size of 3 and a 5-minute duration, then adjusting. Group Deal also doesn't combine with LIVE Flash Sale, Price Cut or the US member price on the same product.
