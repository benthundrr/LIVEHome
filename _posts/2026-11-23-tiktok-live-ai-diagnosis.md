## What changed

TikTok Shop US has published a guide to [AI Diagnosis on LIVE Dashboard](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8161058792818452&default_language=en) on its US Seller University, dated November 20, 2026.

AI diagnosis is an AI-generated review of each LIVE, shown in your LIVE Dashboard. TikTok says it explains how your LIVE performed, which step drove the result and what you can try next time.

Each report has four parts:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| This LIVE performance | Attributed GMV, GMV per hour and impressions per hour, compared with your previous LIVEs and the benchmark |
| What drove this performance | Tap-through rate, average viewing duration, CTR and CTOR, showing which funnel step was strong or weak |
| Performance gaps vs. top performers | What the top 100 LIVEs in your category do that you didn't, with short example clips |
| Key performance clips | Up to three moments where performance rose or dropped, each with what happened and a suggestion |

### How the comparisons work

TikTok defines "previous" as the median of your own LIVEs from the 14 days before this one. The benchmark is the median of LIVEs in your category with both high engagement and high conversion.

## Who it affects

- **US creators who go LIVE** TikTok says all US sellers and creators who go LIVE are eligible, and that all US LIVEs get a report.
- **Shopping LIVE hosts** The headline numbers are sales and traffic metrics, so the report is most useful if you sell in your LIVEs.
- **Teams behind a LIVE** TikTok suggests hosts, producers and agencies use the same report to agree on one or two changes.
{: .cards}

## When it starts

TikTok's guide describes AI diagnosis as available now, with nothing to set up. A report is created for each LIVE automatically.

There are a few limits. Reports are ready about 48 hours after your LIVE ends, they're desktop only, and they come in English, Chinese and Spanish.

TikTok also lists features as coming soon: opening the report from the summary when your LIVE ends, and a new AI diagnosis column in LIVE History.

> **Heads up** The report isn't available in the TikTok Shop Seller app. Open it on desktop through Seller Center or LIVE Manager.

## What LIVE creators should do now

1. **Find the report** On desktop, go to LIVE Manager, then Analytics, then LIVE History, open a LIVE Dashboard and click AI diagnosis.
2. **Check violations first** TikTok says to fix any flagged violation before working on anything else.
3. **Pick the weakest step** Choose the one funnel metric that held you back most.
4. **Try one or two gaps** Watch the example clips from top LIVEs and adapt an idea to your own products and audience.
5. **Compare reports** After your next LIVE, check whether the step you worked on improved.
{: .steps}

TikTok's own example shows how specific the clips can be. In it, products were pinned one to three minutes late and clicks dropped, so the suggestion was to pin products earlier and update sold-out status promptly.
