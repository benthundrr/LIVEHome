## What TikTok LIVE Pro is

LIVE Pro is a recognition TikTok gives to LIVE creators. TikTok Japan's [launch announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/live-pro?lang=ja-JP) from June 9, 2023 describes it as a program that certifies creators who have consistently streamed creative LIVE content as professionals of TikTok LIVE.

TikTok's own LIVE accounts have featured it in other regions too. @tiktoklive_uk posted that [LIVE Pro has arrived](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiktoklive_uk/video/7243722444199021851) on June 12, 2023, and @tiktoklive_us announced a creator as a [LIVE Pro for December](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiktoklive_us/video/7314756249420123434) in December 2023.

- **The badge** TikTok says certified creators get a badge on their profile.
- **Possible extras** TikTok Japan says LIVE Pro creators may get the chance to appear at offline TikTok LIVE events or on TikTok's own accounts.
- **Not permanent** TikTok Japan says LIVE Pro isn't given for good, and the list is added to and reviewed every few months.
- **A gaming version** TikTok runs a separate Gaming LIVE Pro badge for gaming creators.
{: .cards}

### Gaming LIVE Pro

TikTok Japan's [Gaming LIVE Pro announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/gaming-live-pro?lang=ja-JP) from October 17, 2024 says the program started worldwide in March 2024. It says the badge goes to gaming LIVE creators who stream engaging gaming LIVEs and make a big contribution to the TikTok LIVE community, and that it shows during LIVE and on the profile page.

## LIVE Pro requirements: what TikTok has published

TikTok hasn't published a full list of LIVE Pro requirements on its public help, newsroom or LIVE creator pages. What it has said is general.

| Source | What TikTok says |
|---|---|
| LIVE Pro launch, TikTok Japan (2023) | Recognizes creators who consistently stream creative, original LIVE content and draw many fans |
| Gaming LIVE Pro, TikTok Japan (2024) | Goes to gaming creators who stream engaging gaming LIVEs and contribute to the LIVE community |
| Game Crown event, TikTok Japan (2025) | Gaming LIVE Pro has selection criteria, and top finishers aren't guaranteed the badge |

TikTok Japan's [Game Crown announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/ja-jp/tiktok-live-game-crown) said top finishers in that 2025 gaming event could become candidates for Gaming LIVE Pro. It also said Gaming LIVE Pro has its own selection criteria, so placing in the event didn't guarantee the badge.

> **Heads up** Posts and videos that quote exact Diamond, viewer or follower numbers for LIVE Pro are unverified. We couldn't find those numbers on TikTok's public pages, and criteria may differ by region or change.
{: .callout .warn}

TikTok's Gaming LIVE Pro announcement points creators to the Gaming LIVE Pro page in the TikTok app for details. If your app shows a LIVE Pro or Gaming LIVE Pro page, read it there, since that's where TikTok puts the current wording.

### Do you have to be 18 for LIVE Pro?

TikTok hasn't published a separate age rule for LIVE Pro. LIVE Pro recognizes LIVE creators, though, and TikTok's age rules for LIVE are clear.

- **Going LIVE** TikTok's [Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features) say you must be 18 and older to go LIVE.
- **Monetization** The same page says you must be 18 and older to use monetization features.
- **Gifts** TikTok's [LIVE Safety Guide](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/live-safety-guide) says viewers 18 and older, or 19 in South Korea, can access Gifts.
{: .cards}

TikTok's Creator Networks guide [How to Go LIVE on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok) adds that creators must be at least 18, or meet the age of majority in their country or region. The full list of what you need to go LIVE, including followers, is in [TikTok LIVE requirements](/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/).

## How to apply for LIVE Pro

We couldn't find a public LIVE Pro application form on TikTok's pages. TikTok's own announcements describe TikTok certifying creators, adding to the list and reviewing it every few months.

That means the useful steps are the ones in your control. Most tie back to what TikTok has said it looks for.

1. **Meet the LIVE basics** You need to be able to go LIVE first. Check age, followers and region in [TikTok LIVE requirements](/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/).
2. **Keep your account in good standing** A restriction can stop you going LIVE at all. Read [TikTok LIVE ban and restrictions](/blog/tiktok-live-ban-restriction/) if you've had a warning.
3. **Stream consistently** TikTok's launch announcement talks about creators who have streamed creative LIVE content consistently.
4. **Build a real audience** TikTok describes LIVE Pro creators as drawing many fans, so focus on [getting more viewers on TikTok LIVE](/blog/how-to-get-more-viewers-on-tiktok-live/).
5. **Take part in TikTok LIVE events** TikTok has linked at least one gaming event in Japan to Gaming LIVE Pro candidacy, without a guarantee. TikTok hasn't said other events count, but [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/tiktok-live-fest-2026/) is the current big one.
6. **Check your app** If a LIVE Pro or Gaming LIVE Pro page shows in your app, follow what it says.
{: .steps}

> **Tip** Write down your LIVE hours and your best LIVEs each month. If TikTok ever asks for details, or a LIVE Pro page appears in your app, you'll have them ready.

## How to check your LIVE Pro status

TikTok says LIVE Pro creators get a badge on their profile. For Gaming LIVE Pro, TikTok says the badge shows during LIVE and on the profile page.

1. **Open your profile** Look for the LIVE Pro badge on your own TikTok profile.
2. **Check your LIVE room** Go LIVE and look for the badge in your LIVE room. TikTok has said this for Gaming LIVE Pro.
3. **Read your inbox** Check your TikTok notifications and inbox for any message about LIVE Pro. TikTok hasn't published how it tells creators, so this is unverified.
4. **Check the in-app page** If your app shows a LIVE Pro or Gaming LIVE Pro page, it's the place to read current details.
{: .steps}

If you don't see a badge on your profile, you most likely don't have LIVE Pro right now. TikTok hasn't published a status tracker or a way to see how close you are.

> **Heads up** Anyone who asks for money or your password to get you LIVE Pro can't deliver. TikTok decides who gets the badge.
{: .callout .warn}

## Keeping the badge

TikTok Japan's launch announcement says LIVE Pro isn't permanent, and that TikTok plans to add to and review the list every few months. The @tiktoklive_us post naming a creator a LIVE Pro for December also points to a time-limited recognition.

TikTok hasn't published exactly how long the badge lasts or what makes a creator lose it. Treat any fixed timeline you see online as unverified.

> LIVE Pro is TikTok's call, so the steady work that gets you noticed is also what keeps you there.
{: .pull}

What you can control is the same as before: keep streaming, keep your account in good standing and keep your viewers coming back.
