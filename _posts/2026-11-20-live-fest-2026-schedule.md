## LIVE Fest 2026 at a glance

TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 is TikTok's year-end LIVE campaign. TikTok's November 11 announcement, [The Journey to the Global Stage Begins](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en), calls it TikTok LIVE's flagship annual celebration.

These are the dates TikTok has published so far, and where each one comes from.

| Stage | Dates | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-up round | November 6 to 19, 2026 | TikTok's in-app sign-up video |
| Campaign start | November 9, 2026 | TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement |
| Power League | November 20 to December 6, 2026 | [tiktok.com/livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) |
| Fandom League | December 9 to 15, 2026 | tiktok.com/livefest |
| Talent League | Listed in the December 9 to 15 block, with no date line of its own | tiktok.com/livefest |
| Global finals | December 17 to 21, 2026 | tiktok.com/livefest |
| In-app competitions end | End of December 2026 | TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement |
| Regional events | From January 2027 | TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement |
| Global gala | Tokyo, Japan, in 2027 | TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement |

> **Tip** The public page is the overview. The campaign page in your TikTok app shows your track, your rounds and the Event Rules for your region, so check it before you plan a week.

## Where LIVE Fest is right now

Power League opened on November 20 and runs to December 6, according to TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest). TikTok describes it as the league where creators race to climb up the rankings.

Fandom League comes next, from December 9 to 15. TikTok's page describes it as the league where creators connect with their viewers and celebrate community. Talent League, where creators showcase their unique talents, sits in the same block on the page.

The global finals follow from December 17 to 21. TikTok's page calls them the ultimate stage where creators compete to be crowned Creator of 2026.

- **Now** Power League, November 20 to December 6
- **Next** Fandom League and Talent League, from December 9
- **Then** Global finals, December 17 to 21
- **After** Regional events from January 2027, then the Tokyo gala
{: .cards}

How scoring works in each league is covered in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/).

## November 6 or November 9?

You'll see two start dates, and both come from TikTok. TikTok's November 11 newsroom announcement says this year's campaign begins on November 9. TikTok's Japan press announcement, issued November 13, says the in-app ranking event opened on November 6.

The in-app sign-up video lines up with November 6. It says the sign-up round ran from November 6 to 19 and that the leagues open on November 20. TikTok's Event Rules also say the Talent League audition started on November 6.

Sign-up mattered for early points. TikTok's Event Rules say signing up with I'm in awarded early points that carry into the overall Power League leaderboard one-to-one. Creators who missed sign-up can still take part, without those early points. Confirm on your campaign page. ## Dates TikTok hasn't announced

Some dates people search for aren't public yet. Here's what TikTok hasn't published, so you know what not to plan around.

- **Round dates** Power League has three rounds, and Fandom League and Talent League have two each. The day-by-day round calendar is inside the app, and TikTok hasn't published it on its public page.
- **Talent League dates** The public page lists Talent League in the December 9 to 15 block, with no date line of its own.
- **Bonus Hour time** The Power League video labels Bonus Hour 21:00 to 22:00, and TikTok's one-pager fills the time in from the campaign. Confirm the hour and time zone on your campaign page.
- **Regional event dates** TikTok says regional events begin in January 2027 across Southeast Asia, Latin America, Europe and the United States. It hasn't given dates or cities, including for the US.
- **Tokyo gala date and venue** TikTok says the global gala is in Tokyo next year. It hasn't announced the date or venue.
- **How the finals and the gala connect** TikTok's page dates the global finals December 17 to 21, and its announcement calls the Tokyo event the Global LIVE Fest final. TikTok hasn't explained how the two relate.
{: .cards}

> **Heads up** Any post that gives a Tokyo gala date or a US regional date right now is guessing. We'll update this page when TikTok announces them.
{: .callout .warn}

## How past LIVE Fest calendars ran

Earlier campaigns give a rough idea of the rhythm, though the 2026 dates are the ones that count. TikTok's Japan announcement says Tokyo will be the fourth global ceremony, after Miami, London and Las Vegas, and the first in Asia.

| Campaign | Competition start | Global celebration |
|---|---|---|
| LIVE Fest 2024 | Kicked off in October 2024 | London, January 16, 2025 |
| LIVE Fest 2025 | Sign-up from November 7, 2025 | Las Vegas, February 12, 2026 |
| LIVE Fest 2026 | November 6 or 9, 2026 | Tokyo, 2027, date not announced |

TikTok's [2024 recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/celebrating-the-power-of-community-and-creativity-on-tiktok-live-in-2024) says LIVE Fest 2024 kicked off in October 2024 and ended with a global celebration in London on January 16, 2025. TikTok's [2025 kickoff post](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2025-celebrating-growth-community-and-authenticity?lang=en-PH) says creators could sign up from November 7, 2025.

TikTok's [Las Vegas recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) says Global LIVE Fest was held February 12 at Planet Hollywood in Las Vegas. A US Regional LIVE Fest was held there on February 9, and nine regional events ran around the world before the global show.

## What to do before each date

The dates above only help if you plan your LIVEs around them. These steps use what TikTok has published. Confirm each one on your campaign page.

1. **Now, in Power League** Check your track and round dates on your campaign page, and find your Bonus Hour time before you tell viewers a clock time.
2. **Before December 6** TikTok's Event Rules say creators carry 5% of their points from one round to the next, up to a cap that varies by track. Confirm the cap on your campaign page.
3. **Before December 9** Read your Fandom League track. TikTok's Event Rules assign it from your number of active fans as of a date on the campaign page.
4. **Before the global finals** Remind viewers that Votes have to be sent manually. TikTok's rules say unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals.
