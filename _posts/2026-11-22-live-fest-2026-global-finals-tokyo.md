## The short answer

LIVE Fest 2026 ends in two stages. First come the global finals inside the TikTok app, then regional events and a global gala in Tokyo in 2027.

- **Global finals** December 17 to 21, 2026, in the app
- **Regional events** From January 2027, including the United States
- **Global gala** Tokyo, Japan, in 2027
- **Gala date and venue** Not announced yet
{: .cards}

TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9. TikTok Japan's November 13 announcement says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. Both say in-app competitions run through the end of December.

## The global finals, December 17 to 21

TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) lists three dated stages: Power League from November 20 to December 6, Fandom League from December 9 to 15, and the global finals from December 17 to 21. The page describes the finals as the last competition, where creators get a chance to stand out.

The finals happen on TikTok LIVE, so you take part from wherever you stream. TikTok shows who qualifies, how the finals are scored and what you're competing for on your campaign page. Confirm on your campaign page before you plan around any finals rule.

> **Tip** Block out December 17 to 21 now. If you make the finals, your viewers need to know when you'll be LIVE.
{: .callout .tip}

How the leagues feed into that window is in the [Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/), [Fandom League](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/), [Talent League](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/) and [Crossover League](/blog/live-fest-2026-crossover-league/) guides. The full date list is in the [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## The Tokyo gala in 2027

TikTok's newsroom says LIVE Fest 2026 builds to a global gala in Tokyo, Japan next year, and calls it the Global LIVE Fest final. TikTok Japan's announcement adds that this is the fourth global ceremony, and that it will bring LIVE creators from around the world to Tokyo to honor what they achieved this year.

It's also a first for the region. TikTok Japan says earlier global ceremonies were held in Miami, London and Las Vegas, and Tokyo is the first host in Asia.

TikTok Japan says details of the global ceremony will be announced later. Until then, there's no public date, venue, host or lineup.

## Regional events from January 2027

Before Tokyo, TikTok plans regional events. Its announcement says they begin in January 2027 across Southeast Asia, Latin America, Europe and the United States.

Last year's cycle shows how that can look. TikTok's [Las Vegas recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) says nine regional LIVE Fest events ran around the world before the global show, in places such as Dubai, Phuket, Rome, Seoul and Buenos Aires. The US Regional LIVE Fest was held on February 9 at Area 15 in Las Vegas, three days before the global event.

TikTok hasn't said whether the 2027 regional events follow the same pattern. TikTok's Event Rules list an invitation to an offline event as one possible reward. Invitations are limited, each creator can win only one, and no creator is guaranteed one. Confirm on your campaign page.

What the rewards include, and who can take part, is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/).

## What's still unconfirmed

TikTok has given the shape of the finish, but not the details. Treat any specific date or city you see elsewhere with care until TikTok publishes it.

- **Tokyo gala date and venue** TikTok says 2027 and Tokyo, with details to come.
- **Regional event dates and cities** TikTok names the regions, including the United States, with no dates.
- **How the finals and the gala connect** TikTok dates the in-app global finals December 17 to 21 and calls Tokyo the Global LIVE Fest final, but hasn't explained how the two relate.
- **Finals scoring and qualification** These are shown on your campaign page in the app.
