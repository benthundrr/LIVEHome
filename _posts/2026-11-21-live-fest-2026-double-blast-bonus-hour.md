## What Double Blast and Bonus Hour are

Double Blast and Bonus Hour are the two LIVE Fest bonuses that double your points for a limited time. TikTok's Power League one-pager says they can't run at the same time, so they work as two separate windows in a day.

Both sit on top of the base rates in TikTok's Power League video. Regular Gifts are 1 Coin = 1 point, FEST Gifts and Vault Gifts are 1 Coin = 2 points, and 1 Vote = 1 point. Confirm every rate on your campaign page.

The full scoring rules are in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/). Ways to use the bonuses during a week are in [how to get more points in TikTok LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/how-to-get-more-points-live-fest/).

> **Tip** Screenshot the bonus rules on your campaign page at the start of a round. If the page changes, the page is what counts.
{: .callout .tip}

## How Bonus Hour works

Bonus Hour is a daily window of 2x points. While it is on, Gifts and Votes sent during that hour are scored at double the usual rate, according to TikTok's Power League materials. Confirm on your campaign page.

The Power League video labels the window 21:00 to 22:00. TikTok's one-pager fills the clock time in from the campaign, and the time zone can differ by region, so confirm the local time in the app before you tell viewers when to watch.

- **It is daily** Plan around it each day of the round, not as a one-time bonus.
- **It lasts one hour** The video shows a one-hour window. Confirm the start and end in the app.
- **It blocks Double Blast** TikTok says Double Blast can't run while Bonus Hour is active.
- **Viewers need to know** Only support sent during the hour gets the 2x, so say when it starts.
{: .cards}

Power League, which uses this bonus, runs November 20 to December 6, 2026, on TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest). Track placement and rounds are in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) guide.

## How Double Blast works

TikTok's Power League one-pager says Double Blast gives 2x points for 30 minutes. Confirm each of these rules on your campaign page.

1. **Unlock one a day** You can unlock a Double Blast by collecting a Diamond amount shown on the campaign page, or by finishing among the top creators of the day.
2. **Use it the same day** TikTok says it expires the day it is unlocked.
3. **Stay LIVE for the 30 minutes** If you end the LIVE before it expires, the bonus ends and can't be resumed.
4. **Keep it off Bonus Hour** Double Blast can't run while Bonus Hour is active.
{: .steps}

Fandom League has the same 2x for 30 minutes, with one extra rule. TikTok's Fandom materials say a Double Blast unlocked by advancing can be saved for the regional and global Fandom stages. Confirm on your campaign page. Fandom League runs December 9 to 15. How that league works is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Fandom League](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/) guide.

## How to time them

Because the two bonuses can't overlap, the useful move is to treat them as two slots on the same day. Confirm both on your campaign page, and confirm the local Bonus Hour time in the app.

1. **Find your hour** Open the campaign page and write down the Bonus Hour start and end before you post a time to viewers.
2. **Be LIVE when it opens** Join a few minutes early so you're already on when the 2x starts.
3. **Save Double Blast for another stretch** Unlock it earlier in the day, or after Bonus Hour ends, so the 30 minutes don't collide with the hour.
4. **Don't end the LIVE mid-blast** Ending the LIVE ends the 30 minutes, and TikTok says you can't resume it.
5. **Tell the room** Read the bonus out when it starts, so viewers send Gifts and Votes while the 2x is actually running.
{: .steps}

> **Heads up** A clock time copied from a video or a blog can be wrong for your region. Confirm the local time in the app.
{: .callout .warn}

## How the match bonus fits in

The match bonus is a third way TikTok adds points, and it works differently from the 2x timers. Confirm on your campaign page.

In Power League rounds 2 and 3, TikTok's one-pager says you can start more than one LIVE match, and the highest-scoring match of the day is added to your points one-to-one. One strong match counts; extra matches that day don't add a second bonus.

In Fandom League, the rule changes. TikTok's Fandom video says the highest-scoring match of the day that uses the 1-Coin FEST Gift counts as 5x points, and only that match counts.
