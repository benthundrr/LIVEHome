## Start here: the board isn't final until TikTok says so

If your LIVE Fest 2026 points look low, don't panic yet. TikTok's Event Rules say the points shown on a leaderboard may contain errors, and TikTok confirms the final totals after each module ends.

So a number that looks wrong in the middle of a round can still change. Before you go looking for a problem, check the basics below. Every fact here comes from TikTok's own LIVE Fest pages, videos and Event Rules, and every points fact needs a second look on your campaign page.

- **Leaderboards move** TikTok confirms final totals after each module ends
- **Your page is the rulebook** The campaign page in your app shows the rules for your region and track
- **Rates can differ** Confirm every rate and cap on your campaign page
{: .cards}

## Reasons your points didn't count

These are the reasons TikTok's own rules give for points that are missing, lower than expected or taken away.

| What happened | What TikTok's rules say |
|---|---|
| Your app is out of date | An older version of TikTok may miss Event features or may not collect points at all |
| Viewers collected Votes but didn't send them | Votes count only when sent by hand, at 1 Vote = 1 point |
| Double Blast didn't double anything | Double Blast can't run during Bonus Hour |
| Double Blast stopped early | It lasts 30 minutes and ends if you end the LIVE, with no way to resume |
| Your Double Blast disappeared | It expires the same day you unlock it |
| A LIVE Fest video lost its points | Votes earned by a video are deducted if it's removed or set to private before the campaign ends |
| Your points left the board | Opting out takes your points and rankings off the boards until you rejoin |
| One fan's Gifts stopped adding up in Fandom League | One viewer's daily FEST Gift or regular Gift contribution is capped at 300,000 points |
| You missed the sign-up bonus | Creators who skipped sign-up can still take part but don't get the early sign-up points |

Most of these come down to timing and habits, so they're fixable for the next LIVE. The ones that take points away, removed videos and opting out, are worth fixing first.

> **Tip** Write down your Bonus Hour start and end from the campaign page before you plan Double Blast. Confirm the local time in the app, since the 21:00 to 22:00 label in TikTok's Power League video isn't a universal clock.

## Votes, Gifts and the bonus windows

### Votes have to be sent

TikTok's Event Rules say fans collect Votes from missions in the TikTok app, then send them to a creator manually. One Vote equals one point. TikTok also says unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, and Votes can't be moved between accounts.

If your regulars think Votes go to you on their own, they don't. Share [how fans vote in LIVE Fest](/blog/live-fest-vote-how-fans-support-creators/) with them.

### Double Blast and Bonus Hour can't overlap

TikTok describes Bonus Hour as a daily window of 2x points. TikTok's Power League one-pager says Double Blast also gives 2x points, for 30 minutes, with one unlock a day. Double Blast can't run while Bonus Hour is active, and it ends if you end the LIVE.

That means two separate windows a day. The full breakdown is in [Double Blast and Bonus Hour in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-2026-double-blast-bonus-hour/).

### The Fandom League Gift cap

In Fandom League, which TikTok's Fandom video dates December 9 to 15, one viewer's daily contribution from FEST Gifts or regular Gifts is capped at 300,000 points. Support from many fans counts for more than one big gifter. See [the Fandom League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/).

### Talent League has content rules

TikTok's rules say that once you confirm a Talent League track, your LIVE has to keep showing that talent, or you may be removed from Talent League. Gaming has a standard that at least 50% of the LIVE features gaming content. Details are in [the Talent League guide](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/).

## What can cost you every point

Some problems don't just lower a score. TikTok's rules let it block access to the campaign, which can wipe your points.

- **Fake engagement** Bots, agents, third-party plugins or any method used to fake engagement or collect points
- **More than one account** Taking part with a second account
- **Hiding your location** A VPN or another tool that hides your location fails the eligibility rules
- **Missing a reward deadline** Failing to claim a reward on time can forfeit that reward
{: .cards}

> **Heads up** Nobody outside TikTok can sell you points, Votes or a shortcut up a board. TikTok runs the leaderboards. ## What to do when your points look wrong

Work through these in order before your next LIVE.

1. **Update TikTok** Install the latest version, since TikTok's rules say an older one may not collect points.
2. **Reopen your campaign page** Check your league, track, round dates and the rates and caps for your region.
3. **Check your bonus windows** Confirm Bonus Hour in the app and keep Double Blast outside it.
4. **Remind viewers to send Votes** A short reminder during your LIVE helps more than you'd think.
5. **Keep LIVE Fest videos public** Don't delete or hide a video that earned Votes before the campaign ends.
6. **Wait for the module to close** TikTok confirms the final totals after each module ends.
{: .steps}

If something still looks wrong after the module closes, contact TikTok through the app. For how scoring works from the ground up, read [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/), then [how to get more points in TikTok LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/how-to-get-more-points-live-fest/).
