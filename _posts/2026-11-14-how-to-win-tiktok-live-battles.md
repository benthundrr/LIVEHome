## How LIVE Battles and points work

A LIVE Battle is what creators call a [LIVE match](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-battle-explained). TikTok describes it as "a co-host format where creators compete individually or as a team," in a solo match or a team match.

TikTok's [LIVE Studio help](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Start-a-LIVE-match-to-call-on-your-supporters) lays out the basics:

| Rule | What TikTok says |
|---|---|
| Points | Each Coin is one match point, and each like is three |
| Length | The match runs for five minutes |
| Winner | The creator or team with the most points |
| Specific-gift match | Only the chosen Gift adds points |
| Leaving early | The creator who leaves or ends the match loses |
| Draw | Both creators go back to the co-host scene |
| Victory lap | Three minutes, and the winner can give the loser a task |

TikTok's Creator Networks page adds that eligible interactions count "depending on the match settings." So check the match type before you start.

### Starting a match

1. **Start a co-host LIVE** During your LIVE, connect with another creator using co-host.
2. **Tap Match** Tap Match at the bottom of the screen.
3. **Pick the type** Choose Solo match or Team match when it's offered.
4. **Send the invite** The match starts once the other creator accepts.
{: .steps}

In a multi-host match, you can co-host with up to three other creators. Each team's points are the combined points of its members.

## Picking opponents

TikTok's [co-host help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/cohost-on-tiktok-live) gives you two ways in. Tap Send to invite a random recommended creator, or tap Invite to ask a friend, someone you may know, or another recommended creator.

TikTok says recommended creators are suggested based on basic account information, such as app language or follower count, plus their past LIVE content. A close match is usually more fun to watch than a blowout, for both audiences.

- **Similar audience size** Matches stay tense when both rooms can compete.
- **A shared language** Both audiences can follow the banter.
- **A good sport** The victory lap task should stay fun and within the Community Guidelines.

## Power-ups

TikTok's [Power-ups guide](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/article/match-power-ups-win-with-fewer-gifts) lists four Power-ups. When a creator wins a match, that creator's top 3 gifters have a chance to get a random one.

| Power-up | What it does |
|---|---|
| Boosting Glove | For 30 seconds, each Gift has a 30% chance of being worth 5x the normal points |
| Magic Mist | Hides your points from the opponent for 30 seconds |
| Stun Hammer | Adds playful effects to the opponent's LIVE room for 30 seconds |
| Time-Maker | Adds 10 seconds to the match, up to 3 per match |

Power-ups sit in the viewer's backpack and expire 5 days after they're received. A viewer can only use one in a LIVE hosted by the same creator who hosted the LIVE where they got it.

## Pacing and the final 30 seconds

The end of a match is where Power-ups matter most. TikTok's own strategy tip is to use Magic Mist in the last 30 seconds, so the opponent can't tell whether they're ahead.

TikTok also suggests using all three Time-Makers early to control the timing, so the opponent can't add time later. Pick one plan and tell your supporters which one you're running.

> **Tip** Don't end the match early to save a loss. TikTok counts the creator who ends or leaves the match as the loser.
{: .callout .tip}

## Getting gifters ready before you start

Your supporters can only help if they know a match is coming. TikTok's [schedule guide](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Before-you-go-LIVE/Learn-to-reach-more-viewers-with-a-schedule) tells creators to put their LIVE schedule on their profile, set a LIVE Event, and post a teaser video.

- **Say when** Announce the match time in your teaser and on your LIVE.
- **Say which Gift** In a specific-gift match, tell viewers which Gift counts, since other Gifts won't add points.
- **Remind Power-up holders** Power-ups expire after 5 days and only work in your LIVEs.
- **Thank them after** TikTok recognizes the top three contributors on each side during the victory lap.
{: .cards}

## Mistakes to avoid

Battles are competitive, and TikTok's rules still apply in full. Its [LIVE Monetization Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/rules_and_guidance/live_monetization_guidelines) restrict content that pressures viewers through emotions, sympathy or a sense of obligation to send Gifts.

- **Emotional pressure** Asking viewers to gift because you'll be upset if you lose.
- **Leaving mid-match** Ending early hands the win to your opponent.
- **Ignoring your guests** TikTok holds creators accountable for content from everyone in a multi-guest LIVE.
- **Off-limits victory lap tasks** Keep any task within the Community Guidelines.
{: .cards}
