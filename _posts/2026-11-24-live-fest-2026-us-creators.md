## The short answer

Yes, US creators can join TikTok LIVE Fest 2026. The United States, including Puerto Rico, is on the region list in TikTok's in-app Event Rules. TikTok also named the US in two parts of the campaign that only some markets get.

- **Eligible region** The United States, including Puerto Rico, per the in-app Event Rules
- **Creator Academy** TikTok is bringing it back in the United States, Vietnam, Indonesia and Latin America
- **US regional event** TikTok says regional events begin in January 2027 and include the United States
- **Global gala** Tokyo, Japan in 2027, after the regional events
{: .cards}

This page covers the US side of LIVE Fest. The full eligibility checklist and conduct rules are in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/), and the basics of the campaign are in [what TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 is and how to join](/blog/tiktok-live-fest-2026/).

## Who can join LIVE Fest 2026 in the US

Living in the US is the first box to tick. TikTok's in-app Event Rules list every requirement, and you need all of them.

| Requirement | What the Event Rules say |
|---|---|
| Age | At least 18 |
| Region | You live in a listed region. The United States, including Puerto Rico, is on the list |
| LIVE access | Creators must already meet TikTok's requirements to go LIVE |
| Account | A personal account. Business Accounts, Organizational Accounts, accounts used to promote an e-commerce business, and government, politician or political party accounts aren't eligible |
| Location | No VPN or other tool that hides where you are |
| Conduct | You follow TikTok's User Terms and the Event Code of Conduct the whole time |

Meeting the list doesn't guarantee a place. TikTok can deny or end access if it believes you broke the rules.

If you can't go LIVE yet, start with [TikTok LIVE requirements](/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/). If your LIVE access is restricted, read [TikTok LIVE bans and restrictions](/blog/tiktok-live-ban-restriction/) before the campaign moves on without you.

> **Heads up** A VPN set to another country, or a second account, breaks the Event Rules. Stay on one account, in the US, with your real location.
{: .callout .warn}

Fans have a region too. The rules set a supporter's region from the device location of their first Gift after the regional competition begins, and it stays fixed for the rest of LIVE Fest 2026. If your viewers want to know how to help, share [how fans vote and support a creator in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-vote-how-fans-support-creators/).

## How US creators join and when it runs

You sign up for LIVE Fest in the TikTok app, on the LIVE Fest campaign page. There's no separate US sign-up.

1. **Update the app** The rules say an older version of TikTok may miss features or may not collect points at all.
2. **Open the campaign page** Find LIVE Fest in the TikTok app, or start from [tiktok.com/livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest).
3. **Read the Event Rules** Check that the United States is on the region list and that your account type is eligible.
4. **Sign up** Tap the sign-up button on the campaign page, then go LIVE on the schedule you've planned.
{: .steps}

TikTok's [November 11 announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9. TikTok Japan's [announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. TikTok's LIVE Fest page lists these stages:

| Stage | Dates on TikTok's LIVE Fest page |
|---|---|
| [Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) | November 20 to December 6 |
| [Fandom League](/blog/live-fest-2026-fandom-league/) | December 9 to 15 |
| [Talent League](/blog/live-fest-2026-talent-league/) | Listed beside Fandom League, with no separate date |
| Global finals | December 17 to 21 |

The US spans several time zones, so read every time on your campaign page as it shows on your phone. That goes double for Bonus Hour: confirm in the app what local time it runs for you. The full calendar is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## The LIVE Fest Creator Academy in the US

TikTok's November 11 announcement says it is bringing back the LIVE Fest Creator Academy in participating markets across the United States, Vietnam, Indonesia and Latin America. The goal is to help creators prepare for the competition.

TikTok describes the Academy as creator-led LIVE sessions. Creators share practical tips, their own experiences and advice on building engaging LIVE content and stronger connections with an audience.

TikTok hasn't published a 2026 session schedule. Watch TikTok LIVE in the app and the [@tiktok_livefest](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiktok_livefest) account for sessions.

> **Tip** This summer's Community Fest Academy gives a sense of the format. TikTok's [June 8 post](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/community-fest-2026-celebrates-connection-and-discovery-on-tiktok-live?lang=en) says its sessions streamed on @tiktok_livefest and @tiktoklive_us, with featured US TikTok LIVE creators leading them.
{: .callout .tip}

## The US regional event in 2027

TikTok says regional events begin in January 2027 across Southeast Asia, Latin America, Europe and the United States, ahead of the global gala in Tokyo, Japan. That's all TikTok has said about the US event so far.

The in-app rules say each creator can win only one offline invitation, and nobody is guaranteed one. Costs the invitation doesn't cover are on the creator. More on that is in [LIVE Fest 2026 prizes and rewards](/blog/live-fest-2026-prizes-rewards/).
