## Check your eligibility first

An agency can only help you if you can go LIVE and collect Diamonds. TikTok's [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) sets the rules for LIVE Gifts.

- **Age** You must be at least 18, or 19 in South Korea.
- **Location** You must live where LIVE Gifts are available.
- **LIVE access** You must be eligible to go LIVE.
- **Good standing** Your account must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
{: .cards}

TikTok says [LIVE access](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/how-to-get-live-access-on-tiktok) depends on account activity, region and platform policies. On LIVE Studio, you can check your status under 'Your LIVE access'.

## How joining works

Joining a TikTok LIVE agency, which TikTok calls a Creator Network, happens inside the TikTok app. TikTok's [agency invite page](https://www.tiktok.com/tcn/agency_invite) explains the flow: an agent uses your invitation code to send an invitation, and you read and accept it.

1. **Apply at thundrr.co/join** Start your application with Thundrr.
2. **Find your invitation code** In TikTok, go to Settings and privacy, Creator tools, LIVE Center, Agency Center.
3. **Share your code** The agency uses it to send you an invitation.
4. **Accept in the app** Read the invitation, check it's from Thundrr, and accept it.
{: .steps}

> **Tip** Thundrr's official TikTok account is @thundrr.co. Make sure the invitation matches before you accept.
{: .callout .tip}

## Staying safe while you join

A real invitation never needs your password, a payment or an off-app link. TikTok says it [will never ask for your account details](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/avoid-fraudulent-message-attacks-on-tiktok).

> **Heads up** If anyone asks for a fee, your password or a login code to get you into an agency, stop and report it.
{: .callout .warn}
