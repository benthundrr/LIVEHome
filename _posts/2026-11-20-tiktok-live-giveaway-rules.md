## What changed

TikTok Shop US has published a new guide for creators, [Giveaways and Promotional Claims: Creator Best Practices Guide](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7927889548216071&default_language=en), posted to TikTok Shop's US Seller University on November 17, 2026.

TikTok says the guide helps creators tell permitted giveaways apart from prohibited games of chance in US TikTok Shop LIVEs and short videos. It also covers purchase-based incentives, randomized rewards and unsupported claims.

The headline for LIVE creators is simple. Giveaways go through TikTok Shop's official LIVE Giveaway feature, and anything that looks like gambling stays off your LIVE.

## Who it affects

- **Shopping LIVE creators** Anyone selling or promoting products in a US TikTok Shop LIVE.
- **Short video creators** The same rules apply to TikTok Shop short videos.
- **Creators who run giveaways** Including giveaways a seller asks you to host.
- **Card break streams** TikTok says only approved sellers can host card breaks, using transparent, standard formats.
{: .cards}

## When it starts

The guide is live now. It doesn't list a separate start date, so treat it as current guidance for your next LIVE.

TikTok says content that breaks these rules may be subject to enforcement actions, such as content removal or account restrictions. Separate federal, state and local laws may also apply, and TikTok says creators are responsible for making sure their promotions comply.

## The rules at a glance

| Format | What TikTok says |
|---|---|
| Free giveaway | Free to enter, official LIVE Giveaway feature, item valued below $500, clear terms |
| Free gift with purchase | Guaranteed for every qualifying customer, shown in the product listing, total value below $500 |
| Cash or gift cards | Not allowed in either format |
| Wheels, lucky draws, dice, randomizers | Not allowed to pick a winner, gift or product |
| Pay or buy for a chance to win | Not allowed |
| Gambling-style language | Avoid it, even when nobody pays or wins anything |

### On-screen terms for purchase gifts

For a purchase-based incentive in a LIVE, TikTok says to display clear on-screen terms, including:

- whether the free item may arrive separately or later
- any stock or quantity limits
- eligibility criteria
- the promotion's start and end time

TikTok also says creators should check with the seller that the listing and promotion setup are complete, without taking on seller-only listing, configuration or fulfillment work.

> **Heads up** Words matter. TikTok's guide says language that hypes up financial return or implies gambling can trigger a violation even when the activity itself is permitted. "Free gift with every qualifying order" is fine. "Buy now and you could win" isn't.

## What LIVE creators should do now

1. **Drop the wheel** Retire any spin wheel, lucky draw, dice game or randomizer app you use to pick winners or gifts.
2. **Use the official feature** Run free giveaways only through TikTok Shop's LIVE Giveaway feature, free to enter and under $500.
3. **Rewrite your scripts** Swap "win" and "chance" wording for plain, guaranteed wording that matches the listed offer.
4. **Add on-screen terms** For gifts with purchase, show the timing, limits, eligibility and start and end time on screen.
5. **Confirm with the seller** Check the listing and promotion setup before you go LIVE.
6. **Run TikTok's self-check** The guide ends with a checklist to go through before you publish or go LIVE.
