## What you get going solo

You don't need an agency to go LIVE on TikTok. TikTok's Help Center page [What is TikTok LIVE?](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/what-is-tiktok-live) lists the requirements: you must be at least 18, and you need 1,000 followers to go LIVE, which may vary across regions.

To collect Diamonds, TikTok's [LIVE Gifts page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) says you must live where LIVE Gifts is available, be 18 or older (19 in South Korea), be eligible to go LIVE and have an account in good standing. An agency isn't on either list.

### TikTok's own tools are open to you

- **LIVE Gifts** Viewers send Gifts, and TikTok says it awards Diamonds the more popular your LIVE becomes.
- **LIVE matches** TikTok's [LIVE match page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-match) describes a co-host mode where creators compete solo or in teams, and the most points wins.
- **LIVE Events** TikTok says [LIVE Events](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/tiktok-live-events) let you announce a LIVE in advance so viewers can register and get notified.
- **LIVE Campaigns** TikTok runs [LIVE Campaigns](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/tiktok-live-campaign) periodically, with missions, rewards and leaderboards you can find under Activities.

### What solo really means

Going solo gives you full control. You choose your schedule, your content and every decision about your account.

It also means you do all of it yourself: planning, learning the tools, reading TikTok's policies and working out what to try next. ## What a TikTok LIVE agency adds

On TikTok, a LIVE agency is called a Creator Network. TikTok's guide [What Are TikTok LIVE Creator Networks?](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide) describes them as third parties independent from TikTok that support creators through LIVE operations, educational resources and development guidance.

TikTok lists the kinds of support a network may offer. It also says support depends on each network's model and regional requirements, so what you actually get varies.

- **Creator development** Educational resources, onboarding support and guidance on LIVE quality and consistency.
- **LIVE operations** LIVE planning, scheduling coordination and moderation guidance.
- **Performance insights** Data that helps you understand audience engagement patterns.
- **Policy guidance** Help understanding TikTok's policies and account practices.
{: .cards}

TikTok also says many networks encourage community, where creators exchange experiences and share best practices. For a full explainer, read [what a TikTok LIVE agency is](/blog/what-is-a-tiktok-live-agency/).

## Costs and trade-offs

An agency's value depends on its terms. Before you accept an invitation, check what you're agreeing to.

### What to look at

| Trade-off | What to check |
|---|---|
| Fees | Whether the agency charges anything to join or to stay |
| Your Diamonds | Whether the agency asks for any share of your earnings |
| Contract | Whether there's a contract, how long it runs and how you can leave |
| Control | Whether you still decide your own schedule and content |
| Support | Who your contact is and how you reach them |

TikTok's Creator Networks site says networks can [manage contracts](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en) on LIVE Backstage, the platform TikTok provides to networks. So read any terms in full before you accept.

For more on money, read [Do TikTok LIVE agencies take your Diamonds?](/blog/do-tiktok-live-agencies-take-your-diamonds/) and our guide to [TikTok Diamonds to USD](/blog/tiktok-diamonds-to-usd/).

### Vet the agency itself

TikTok notes that Creator Networks are independent businesses and that it doesn't endorse or take responsibility for their business conduct. That makes checking who you're dealing with your job.

> **Heads up** No agency can grant LIVE access or get you around TikTok's requirements. If someone promises that, or asks for your password, walk away.
