## The short answer

The final round of Power League is round 3. Creators who advanced from round 1 compete in the Legend, Crown and Rising tracks, and TikTok's Power League one-pager in the app says top creators from round 3 advance to the global finals.

Power League runs November 20 to December 6, 2026, on TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest). TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, while TikTok Japan's November 13 [press announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001376.000030435.html) says the in-app ranking event opened November 6.

- **Which round** Round 3, the last of Power League's three rounds
- **Who's in it** Creators in the Legend, Crown and Rising tracks
- **When it ends** Power League closes December 6, 2026
- **What's at stake** Top creators advance to the global finals
{: .cards}

This checklist is for the final days. How the whole league works is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) guide, and every date is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## What changes in the final round

Round 3 keeps the same three tracks as round 2. What changes is how much rides on it. Check your campaign page for each of these.

| What | What TikTok describes | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Tracks | Legend, Crown and Rising continue from round 2 | Which track you're in |
| Carry-over | Creators carry 5% of their points from one round to the next, up to a cap by track | Your track's cap |
| Match bonus | Your highest-scoring LIVE match of the day is added to your points one-to-one in rounds 2 and 3 | Your round dates |
| Advancing | Top creators from round 3 advance to the global finals | Your track's board |

The carry-over is small, so the points you earned in round 2 won't carry you far. Treat the first day of round 3 like a new start and plan your strongest LIVEs early.

TikTok hasn't published how many creators advance from round 3 to the global finals. A post that gives one cutoff for every track is guessing.

> **Heads up** The day-by-day round calendar is in the app only. Don't plan your final round from a date you saw in someone else's post.
{: .callout .warn}

## Before round 3 starts

Do these before the round opens, so the first day isn't spent catching up.

1. **Update the TikTok app** TikTok's Event Rules say an older app version may not collect points.
2. **Check your track and dates** Open the LIVE Fest campaign page and note your round 3 track and its start and end times.
3. **Screenshot the rates** TikTok's Power League video shows Gifts at 1 Coin = 1 point, FEST Gifts and Vault Gifts at 1 Coin = 2 points, and 1 Vote = 1 point. Check your campaign page, since the page is what counts.
4. **Find your Bonus Hour** TikTok describes Bonus Hour as a daily 2x window. Check in the app for the local time before you tell viewers.
5. **Post your LIVE schedule** Tell your regular viewers which days and times you'll be LIVE for the final round.
{: .steps}

## Plan each day of the final round

Each day of round 3 gives you three separate chances to add points. Check your campaign page for every rate and window.

- **Bonus Hour** A daily window of 2x points. Check in the app for your local time, and be LIVE for it.
- **Double Blast** TikTok's one-pager says it's 2x points for 30 minutes, once a day, and it can't run during Bonus Hour. You get one by reaching a Diamond amount shown on your campaign page, or by finishing among the top creators of the day.
- **Your best match** You can start more than one LIVE match in rounds 2 and 3, and only the highest-scoring match of the day is added to your points one-to-one.
{: .cards}

Keep the windows apart. Bonus Hour and Double Blast can't overlap, and TikTok says a Double Blast expires the day you get it and ends if you end the LIVE before the 30 minutes are up.

Tell viewers when each window starts. Only the Gifts and Votes sent during a window get the 2x, so the people watching need to know it's running. ## Votes in the last days

Votes are points your viewers can give without spending Coins. TikTok's Event Rules say viewers collect them through missions and send them by hand, at 1 Vote = 1 point. Check your campaign page.

- **Remind them to send** A Vote sitting unused adds nothing to your board.
- **Explain the deadline** Unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, at the time shown on the campaign page. That means Votes can also count in Fandom League or Talent League.
- **Agree on a plan** Decide with your viewers whether Votes go into the final round or wait for the December leagues.
- **Keep videos up** Votes earned by a video are deducted if that video is removed or set to private before the campaign ends.
{: .cards}

Share [how fans vote and support a creator in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-vote-how-fans-support-creators/) with your viewers, so they know how to send Votes.

## The last day of Power League

Power League ends December 6, 2026. The exact closing time is on your campaign page, so check it before your last LIVE.

1. **Check the closing time** Plan your last Bonus Hour and Double Blast inside the round, not after it ends.
2. **Stay inside the rules** TikTok's rules say a VPN, a second account, bots or fake engagement can get you removed, and removed points are gone.
3. **Don't opt out** Creators who opt out lose their points and rankings from the boards until they rejoin.
4. **Wait for final totals** TikTok's Event Rules say leaderboard points may contain errors, and TikTok settles final totals after each module ends.
{: .steps}
