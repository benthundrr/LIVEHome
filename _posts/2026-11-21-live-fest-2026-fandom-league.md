## What Fandom League is

Fandom League is the LIVE Fest 2026 league where creators, in TikTok's words, connect with their viewers and celebrate community. TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) and the Fandom video both date it from December 9 to 15, 2026.

It comes after Power League, which runs November 20 to December 6, and before the global finals, December 17 to 21. Top creators from Fandom League can compete on the Fandom board at those finals. Every date is in the [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

- **Dates** December 9 to 15, 2026
- **Rounds** Two, with the day-by-day calendar in the app
- **Tracks** Six, assigned from your number of active fans
- **What comes next** Global finals, December 17 to 21
{: .cards}

Power League is still running until December 6. That path is in the [LIVE Fest 2026 Power League](/blog/live-fest-2026-power-league/) guide.

## How tracks and rounds work

TikTok's Fandom video says creators compete in one of six tracks across two rounds. The Event Rules assign those tracks from the number of active fans a creator has, as of a date and time printed on the campaign page. Confirm on your campaign page.

TikTok hasn't published the names of the six tracks, or how many creators advance from each one. Your campaign page in the TikTok app shows the track you were given and the rules that apply to it.

The two rounds sit inside the December 9 to 15 window. TikTok hasn't published a public day-by-day calendar for them, so read the dates in the app before you plan the week.

> **Tip** Check your Fandom track before December 9. The active-fan number TikTok uses is as of a date on the campaign page, and waiting until the league opens leaves less time to plan.
{: .callout .tip}

## How Fandom League scores a LIVE

TikTok's Fandom video shows these base rates. Rates can change during the campaign, so confirm each one on your campaign page.

| What viewers send | Points shown in TikTok's Fandom video |
|---|---|
| FEST Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Regular Gifts | 1 Coin = 1 point |
| Votes | 1 Vote = 1 point |

Gifters also count. The video leaves the gifter rate as a value on the campaign page, so don't assume a number. Confirm on your campaign page.

One viewer's daily contribution through FEST Gifts or regular Gifts is capped at 300,000 points. Confirm on your campaign page. That cap is why a wider group of supporters can matter as much as one big gifter.

Two bonuses sit on top of that base. Confirm both on your campaign page.

- **Match bonus** The highest-scoring match of the day that uses the 1-Coin FEST Gift counts as 5x points, and only that match counts.
- **Double Blast** 2x points for 30 minutes. It ends if you end the LIVE. A Double Blast unlocked by advancing can be saved for the regional and global Fandom stages.
{: .cards}

How Double Blast and Bonus Hour work, including the rule that they can't overlap, is in [Double Blast and Bonus Hour in LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/live-fest-2026-double-blast-bonus-hour/). The full scoring comparison across leagues is in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/).

## How fans help

Votes and Gifts are the two ways viewers add to your Fandom League score. TikTok's Event Rules say 1 Vote = 1 point, and that Votes have to be sent manually during Fandom League, as well as during Power League, Talent League and the global finals. Confirm on your campaign page.

Viewers collect those Votes from missions. During the main competition, TikTok's rules name a daily visit to the campaign page, sending any Gift, a daily Coin recharge, posting with TikTok's LIVE Fest templates, becoming a creator's Guardian, and Votes that can appear in Treasure Boxes during a LIVE. The Vote count for each mission is printed on the campaign page.

1. **Tell viewers to send** A Vote that isn't sent adds nothing. Unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, at the time shown on the page.
2. **Ask them to keep videos up** If a video that earned Votes is removed or set to private before the campaign ends, TikTok says those Votes are deducted.
3. **Remember the Gift cap** One viewer's FEST Gifts or regular Gifts stop adding points after 300,000 points in a day. Confirm on your campaign page.
4. **Plan the 1-Coin FEST Gift match** Only the day's highest-scoring match that uses that Gift gets the 5x bonus.
{: .steps}

Votes can't be moved between accounts. A viewer who opts out in the campaign page settings disappears from supporter lists until they rejoin. More on eligibility and conduct is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/).

> Tell viewers to send the Vote from the campaign screen. A Gift and a Vote are both support, and only the Vote that is sent becomes a point.
{: .pull}

## A prep checklist before December 9

Fandom League opens December 9. These steps use what TikTok has published. Confirm each one on your campaign page.

1. **Read your track** Open the campaign page and find the Fandom track you were assigned, and the active-fan date TikTok used.
2. **Confirm the rates** Check the Gift, Vote and gifter rates on your page, including the 300,000-point daily cap.
3. **Explain Votes** Tell your regular viewers how to collect Votes and that they have to send them manually.
4. **Plan one 1-Coin FEST Gift match a day** The 5x bonus applies only to the highest-scoring match of the day that uses that Gift.
5. **Stay eligible** TikTok can remove you for a VPN, a second account, bots or fake engagement, so fix any of those before the league starts.
{: .steps}

You also have to meet TikTok's [LIVE requirements](/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/) to go LIVE at all.
