## The short answer

You don't need an agency to compete in TikTok LIVE Fest 2026. TikTok's Event Rules tell each creator to sign up on their own campaign page by tapping I'm in, and they don't describe a separate leaderboard for agencies or creator networks.

An agency can still help. A good one helps you read your campaign page, plan your LIVEs around TikTok's dates and explain to your viewers how they can support you. It can't touch your points, your track or your rank.

> TikTok runs the LIVE Fest boards. An agency helps you prepare for them.
{: .pull}

## What TikTok's rules say about agencies

TikTok's Event Rules for LIVE Fest 2026 are written for two groups: creators and viewers. The creator signs up, goes LIVE and earns points. The viewer collects Votes from missions and sends them manually, and Gifts add points from the Coins spent. Confirm on your campaign page.

TikTok's campaign disclaimer says the information is general reference, and that TikTok makes no guarantees about the success of any creator network, creator or LIVE. TikTok's 2026 materials don't publish a ranking for agencies.

The dates come from TikTok too. TikTok's [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, and TikTok Japan's press release gives November 6 for the start of in-app rankings. TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) lists these windows:

| Fandom League | December 9 to 15 |

| Global finals | December 17 to 21 |

## What no agency can do for you

Some promises sound helpful and break TikTok's rules, or can't be kept at all. Based on what TikTok has published, no agency can do any of these:

- **Sign up for you** The Event Rules tell the creator to tap I'm in on their own campaign page.
- **Add points** TikTok scores Gifts and Votes and confirms final totals after each module ends. Confirm on your campaign page.
- **Pick your track** TikTok assigns Power League tracks. Its rules tie placement to your recent Diamonds, so check your campaign page for yours.
- **Promise a rank or prize** TikTok runs the leaderboards, and its disclaimer makes no guarantees for any creator network.
{: .cards}

> **Warning** TikTok's rules let it remove creators for using bots or fake engagement, or for taking part with more than one account. A VPN or another tool that hides your location fails the eligibility rules. Anyone who suggests one of these is risking your campaign.
{: .callout .warn}

## Joining an agency during LIVE Fest

TikTok hasn't published a rule about joining an agency or creator network partway through LIVE Fest. Whether a move changes anything on your campaign page is unconfirmed, so take a screenshot of your page before you accept and check it again after.

1. **Check the invitation** A real invitation arrives inside the TikTok app and never needs your password or a fee.
2. **Read the terms** Ask what the agency charges, whether there's a contract and who your manager will be.
3. **Check your campaign page** Confirm your league, track and points look the same after you join.
{: .steps}
