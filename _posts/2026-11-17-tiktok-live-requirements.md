**Quick answer:** TikTok's Help Center lists 1,000 followers and a minimum age of 18 to go LIVE, and notes that the follower number may vary across regions. LIVE also has to be available where you live, and your account needs to follow TikTok's Community Guidelines.

## What TikTok requires to go LIVE

TikTok's Help Center page [What is TikTok LIVE?](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/what-is-tiktok-live) lists who can use LIVE. Here's what it says you need.

- **Age** You must be at least 18 years old to go LIVE.
- **Followers** You need 1,000 followers to go LIVE, and TikTok notes this may vary across regions.
- **Location** LIVE and Gift features aren't currently available everywhere.
- **Good standing** TikTok asks you to follow its Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
{: .cards}

### The age rule

TikTok's [age requirements page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/age-requirements-for-tiktok-live) says you must be aged 18 or older to go LIVE. TikTok may ask you to confirm your age before you go LIVE, depending on factors such as your location or whether you've already done so.

TikTok bases your LIVE eligibility on your confirmed age. If it can't confirm your age, you won't be able to go LIVE. TikTok's [Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features) repeat the rule: you must be 18 and older to go LIVE. For the badge TikTok gives some LIVE creators, read [how to get the TikTok LIVE Pro badge](/blog/tiktok-live-pro-badge/).

### The follower rule

TikTok's pages don't all give the same detail. The Help Center lists 1,000 followers, with a note that it may vary across regions.

TikTok's Creator Networks guide [How to Go LIVE on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok) says only that creators need the required number of followers, which may vary by region. The [LIVE Safety Guide](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/live-safety-guide) says a certain number of followers, which may vary by market.

> **Tip** Treat 1,000 as TikTok's published guide, and let your own app be the final word. TikTok doesn't publish a single fixed number for every country.
{: .callout .tip}

### Starting out

The LIVE Safety Guide adds that when you first start going LIVE, you may not have access to all the features LIVE has to offer. TikTok says you gain access to the full LIVE experience over time, as long as you follow its safety policies.

## LIVE access vs LIVE Gifts

Going LIVE and collecting Diamonds have separate rules. Meeting the LIVE requirements lets you stream, and LIVE Gifts have their own checklist on top.

TikTok's [LIVE Gifts help page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) says that to turn on LIVE Gifts, you must meet these requirements.

| Requirement | To go LIVE | To turn on LIVE Gifts |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 18 or older | 18 or older (19 in South Korea) |
| Followers | 1,000, may vary across regions | You must be eligible to go LIVE |
| Location | LIVE isn't available everywhere | You must live where LIVE Gifts are available |
| Account | Follow the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service | An account in good standing that follows the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service |

TikTok also notes that government, politician and political party accounts, plus other public interest accounts, can't collect Diamonds through LIVE Gifts.

TikTok's guide to [LIVE monetization requirements](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-monetization-requirements) adds that creators are expected to interact with audiences naturally. It says artificial or misleading engagement practices may affect eligibility for monetization tools.

## Why you can't go LIVE

If the LIVE option is missing or won't start, check these first. Each one comes from TikTok's own help and policy pages.

| Reason | What TikTok says |
|---|---|
| You're under 18 | You must be 18 or older to go LIVE. |
| Your age isn't confirmed | If TikTok can't confirm your age, you won't be able to go LIVE. |
| Not enough followers | The Help Center lists 1,000 followers, which may vary across regions. |
| LIVE isn't available where you are | LIVE and Gift features aren't currently available everywhere. |
| A rule violation | If a LIVE breaks TikTok's policies, the session may be stopped and you could face temporary restrictions from going LIVE. |

The Community Guidelines also say repeated or serious violations, including attempts to bypass restrictions, may result in an account ban. If you've had a LIVE cut short, read the notice TikTok sent you before you try again.

### Where to check your status

1. **Find LIVE in the app** TikTok's Help Center says to tap the Add post + button, then tap LIVE at the bottom. You may need to scroll across.
2. **Run Account check** In the TikTok app, tap Profile, tap the Menu button, select TikTok Studio, then tap Account check under More tools. TikTok's [account status page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/account-status) says a warning sign means there's an issue.
3. **Check LIVE Studio on desktop** TikTok's guide to [LIVE access](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/how-to-get-live-access-on-tiktok) says to open LIVE Studio and select 'Your LIVE access' in the top-right corner to see your status and application options.
{: .steps}

### Applying for LIVE Studio access

TikTok says LIVE access on LIVE Studio depends on account activity, region and platform policies. For active LIVE creators, it becomes available once the account meets criteria including account history, recent LIVE activity, follower requirements and good standing.

If you're not active on TikTok LIVE or you're coming from another platform, TikTok says 'Your LIVE access' opens an application page with a form. TikTok reviews it and sends a notification once it makes a decision.

> **Heads up** Anyone who promises to unlock LIVE for a fee, or asks for your password to do it, can't deliver. TikTok makes LIVE access decisions itself.
{: .callout .warn}
