## What earns points

Every tactic below comes from the scoring TikTok has published in its LIVE Fest campaign videos and Event Rules. Rates can change during the campaign, so confirm each one on your campaign page in the TikTok app.

TikTok's Power League video, which runs November 20 to December 6, shows these rates:

| What viewers send | Points shown in TikTok's Power League video |
|---|---|
| Regular Gifts | 1 Coin = 1 point |
| FEST Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Vault Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Votes | 1 Vote = 1 point |

On top of that base, TikTok names three bonuses: Bonus Hour, Double Blast and the match bonus. The full scoring rules for every league are in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/). This guide is about using them.

> **Tip** Screenshot the rates on your campaign page at the start of each round. If the page changes, the page is what counts.
{: .callout .tip}

## Plan your LIVEs around the bonuses

Bonus Hour and Double Blast both double your points, and TikTok says they can't overlap. That gives you two separate windows a day to plan for. Confirm both on your campaign page.

- **Go LIVE for Bonus Hour** TikTok describes Bonus Hour as a daily window of 2x points. The Power League video labels it 21:00 to 22:00, so check the time and time zone on your campaign page before you tell viewers.
- **Save Double Blast for a strong stretch** TikTok's one-pager says Double Blast is 2x points for 30 minutes. You can unlock one a day by reaching a Diamond amount shown on the page, or by finishing among the top creators of the day.
- **Use it the same day** TikTok says Double Blast expires the day it's unlocked.
- **Stay LIVE until it ends** If you end the LIVE before the 30 minutes are up, TikTok says the bonus ends and can't be resumed.
{: .cards}

Tell your viewers when a bonus starts. Only the Gifts and Votes sent during the window get the 2x, so the people watching need to know it's running.

## Get your viewers sending Votes

Votes are points your viewers can give without spending Coins. TikTok's Event Rules say viewers collect Votes by completing missions, and 1 Vote = 1 point. Confirm on your campaign page.

1. **Explain where Votes come from** TikTok's rules name missions such as a daily visit to the campaign page, sending a Gift, posting with TikTok's LIVE Fest templates and becoming a creator's Guardian. Votes can also appear in Treasure Boxes during a LIVE.
2. **Remind them to send** TikTok's rules say Votes must be sent manually during Power League, Fandom League, Talent League and the global finals. A Vote sitting unused adds nothing.
3. **Mention the deadline** Unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals, at the time shown on the campaign page.
4. **Keep their videos up** If a video that earned Votes is removed or set to private before the campaign ends, TikTok says those Votes are deducted.
{: .steps}

Votes can't be moved between accounts. More on how fans support creators is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/).

## Make one match a day count

LIVE matches matter from Power League round 2. TikTok's one-pager says that in rounds 2 and 3 you can start more than one match, and the highest-scoring match of the day is added to your points one-to-one. Confirm on your campaign page.

That means one strong match beats several average ones. Pick a time when your regular viewers are on, and let them know the match is coming.

Fandom League, December 9 to 15, scores matches differently. TikTok's Fandom video says the highest-scoring match of the day that uses the 1-Coin FEST Gift counts as 5x points, and only that match counts. Confirm on your campaign page.

## Get ready for Fandom and Talent League

The December leagues reward different things, so plan for them before Power League ends. Confirm each rate on your campaign page.

- **Fandom League** TikTok caps one viewer's daily contribution from FEST Gifts or regular Gifts at 300,000 points. A wide group of supporters counts for more than relying on a single big gifter.
- **Fandom tracks** TikTok's Event Rules assign Fandom tracks from your number of active fans, as of a date and time on the campaign page.
- **Talent League** TikTok's Talent video scores Music and Dancing FEST Gifts at 1 Coin = 2 points, 1 gifter = 100 points and 100 viewers = 40 points. For Gaming, it shows regular Gifts at 1 Coin = 1 point, FEST Gifts at 1 Coin = 2 points, 1 gifter = 100 points and 1 Super Fan = 500 points.
- **Take the Stage** For Music and Dancing, TikTok's rules say the highest-scoring Take the Stage of the day counts as 5x points.
{: .cards}

Once your Talent track is confirmed, TikTok's rules say your LIVE has to keep showing that talent, or you may be removed from Talent League. Stay on the track you picked.

Dates for each league are in the [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## Protect the points you earn

Points you lose to a rule break are gone. TikTok's Event Rules let it block or end your access to the campaign for any of these.

- **Faking engagement** Bots, agents, third-party plugins or any method used to fake engagement or collect points.
- **More than one account** Taking part with more than one account is listed on its own line.
- **Hiding your location** Using a VPN or another tool that hides your location fails TikTok's eligibility rules.
- **Breaking LIVE rules** A LIVE that breaks TikTok's rules can cost you access.
{: .cards}

TikTok's LIVE feature guidelines also name asking for Gifts through overly repetitive activities, automatic systems or programmed loops as a behavior that can affect your LIVE features. Ask for support in your own words, and keep it natural.

> **Heads up** If you opt out of LIVE Fest, TikTok says your points and rankings come off the boards until you rejoin. Giveaways on LIVE have their own rules too, covered in [TikTok LIVE giveaway rules](/blog/tiktok-live-giveaway-rules/).
