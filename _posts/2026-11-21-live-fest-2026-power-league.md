## What Power League is

Power League is the first league of TikTok LIVE Fest 2026. TikTok's [LIVE Fest page](https://www.tiktok.com/livefest) dates it from November 20 to December 6, 2026, and describes it as the league where creators compete to climb the rankings.

It's built on points. Your viewers' Gifts and Votes turn into points, and TikTok ranks creators on leaderboards inside each track. TikTok's Event Rules say top creators receive rewards and honor titles.

- **Dates** November 20 to December 6, 2026
- **Rounds** Three, with the day-by-day calendar in the app
- **Later tracks** Legend, Crown and Rising
- **What comes next** Global finals, December 17 to 21
{: .cards}

Power League sits inside a longer campaign. TikTok's November 11 [newsroom announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-fest-2026-the-journey-to-the-global-stage-begins-us?lang=en) says the campaign began November 9, while TikTok's Japan announcement says the in-app ranking event opened November 6. Every date is in the [TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 schedule](/blog/live-fest-2026-schedule/).

## How TikTok places you in a round 1 track

TikTok splits creators into tracks before Power League starts. Its Event Rules place creators in the same region by the highest number of Diamonds they collected in any single month during a six-month window, as of a date printed in the rules. Confirm on your campaign page.

The number of round 1 tracks changes by region, so there's no single list that fits everyone. Your campaign page in the TikTok app shows the track you were given.

> **Tip** Your track is a starting group. The points you collect during each round decide who moves on, so read your track and round dates before you plan the week.
{: .callout .tip}

Early points count here too. TikTok's Event Rules say the early points from signing up with I'm in carry into the overall Power League leaderboard one-to-one. Creators who missed sign-up can still take part, without those points. Confirm on your campaign page. [What TikTok LIVE Fest 2026 is and how to join](/blog/tiktok-live-fest-2026/) covers sign-up.

## Round 1 to round 3: Legend, Crown and Rising

TikTok's Power League one-pager in the app lays out the path. This is the order TikTok describes. The dates for each round are on your campaign page.

1. **Round 1** You compete in the track you were placed in. How many creators advance depends on the track, and TikTok hasn't published one cutoff for everyone.
2. **Round 2** Creators who advance merge into three tracks: Legend, Crown and Rising. LIVE matches start to count, through the match bonus.
3. **Round 3** The same three tracks continue. Top creators from Legend, Crown and Rising advance to the global finals.
{: .steps}

Points can follow you between rounds. TikTok's Event Rules say creators carry 5% of their points from one round to the next, up to a cap that varies by track. The cap is printed under that rule. Confirm on your campaign page.

Rising has a track history. TikTok's [Las Vegas recap](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-live-celebrates-creators-at-global-live-fest-event-in-las-vegas?lang=en) lists Power League and Power League Rising Track among the seven LIVE Fest 2025 categories, with the top creator in each taking home that track's title.

## How Power League points add up

TikTok's Power League video shows these base rates. Rates can change during the campaign, so confirm each one on your campaign page.

| What viewers send | Points shown in TikTok's Power League video |
|---|---|
| Regular Gifts | 1 Coin = 1 point |
| FEST Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Vault Gifts | 1 Coin = 2 points |
| Votes | 1 Vote = 1 point |

TikTok names three bonuses on top of that base. Confirm each on your campaign page.

- **Bonus Hour** A daily window of 2x points. The video labels it 21:00 to 22:00, so check the hour and time zone on your page.
- **Double Blast** 2x points for 30 minutes, one unlock a day. It ends if your LIVE ends, and it can't run during Bonus Hour.
- **Match bonus** In rounds 2 and 3, your highest-scoring LIVE match of the day is added to your points one-to-one.
{: .cards}

The match bonus is the Power League rule that changes how you plan the later rounds. One strong match a day counts for more than several average ones, so pick a time when your regular viewers are on.

The full scoring rules are in [how LIVE Fest 2026 points and rankings work](/blog/live-fest-2026-rankings/). Tactics for each bonus are in [how to get more points in TikTok LIVE Fest 2026](/blog/how-to-get-more-points-live-fest/).

## From Power League to the global finals

TikTok's LIVE Fest page dates the global finals from December 17 to 21, 2026, and calls them the stage where creators compete to be crowned Creator of 2026. Top creators from Power League, Fandom League and Talent League compete there on global boards inside their tracks.

TikTok's Event Rules list Power: Legend, Power: Crown and Power: Rising among the global track names. They also say a creator may be listed on up to three boards at once.

Scoring shifts slightly at the finals. The rules add a line for the global finals: Vault Gifts sent in combo are 1 Coin = 4 points. Confirm on your campaign page.

Votes matter all the way through. TikTok's rules say Votes have to be sent manually, and unused Votes expire at the end of the global finals. Who can take part, and what can remove you, is in [LIVE Fest 2026 eligibility, prizes and rules](/blog/live-fest-2026-rules/).

What happens once Power League closes on December 6 is in [what happens after LIVE Fest Power League ends](/blog/after-live-fest-power-league/).

## What TikTok hasn't confirmed

Some Power League details people search for aren't public. TikTok shows them in the app, or hasn't published them at all.

- **Round 1 advance counts** TikTok says the number that advances depends on the track. The number for your track is on your campaign page.
- **Round dates** Power League has three rounds, and the day-by-day calendar is in the app only.
- **Which track feeds which** TikTok hasn't published how round 1 tracks map onto Legend, Crown and Rising.
- **Carry-over cap** The 5% carry-over has a cap that varies by track, shown on your campaign page.
{: .cards}

> **Heads up** A post that gives an exact round 1 cutoff for every track, or says which creators land in Rising, is guessing. Your campaign page is the source that counts.
