## Why there is no single best agency

Creators searching for the best TikTok LIVE agencies run into a simple truth quickly: TikTok does not publish an agency leaderboard, and the right network depends on how often you stream, where you live, and what help you want.

In TikTok's own documentation, a LIVE agency is called a Creator Network. TikTok's [guide to Creator Networks](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide) defines them as third parties independent from TikTok that support creators with livestream operations, educational resources, and development guidance.

- **Independent businesses** TikTok reviews organizations that apply through LIVE Backstage, but it states it does not endorse or sponsor any network.
- **Different specialties** Some networks focus on daily coaching and scheduling, others on LIVE Battles, gaming, or music, and each operates only in approved regions.
- **Your terms vary** Fees, contracts, and hour expectations are set by each network, not by TikTok.

## The 2026 checklist for comparing agencies

Before you accept an invitation from any TikTok LIVE agency, check these five areas side by side and ask for the answers in writing.

| Check | Question to ask | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Fees and Diamonds | Do you charge any fee or take any percentage of my Diamonds? | Zero joining fee and zero cut of Diamonds |
| Contracts and exit | Is there a lock-in period, minimum hours, or exit notice? | Clear exit terms before you accept |
| Creator support | Who is my point of contact and how often do we talk? | A dedicated creator manager and clear response channels |
| LIVE Fest and events | How do you support LIVE Battles and TikTok events? | Structured coaching, scheduling, and campaign guidance |
| Region match | Are you approved to invite creators in my country? | Active operations in your exact territory and language |

### Check fees and Diamond terms first

Never assume an agency is free just because another creator told you agencies do not charge. Ask directly whether there is a setup fee, monthly fee, or deduction from your Diamonds, and read [do TikTok LIVE agencies take your Diamonds](/blog/do-tiktok-live-agencies-take-your-diamonds/) before accepting.

### Check contracts and hour minimums

TikTok allows Creator Networks to manage agreements through [LIVE Backstage](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/what-is-tiktok-live-backstage), which means some networks use fixed-term contracts or monthly streaming minimums while others have none. If an agency requires 15 to 60 hours a month or a notice period to leave, decide whether that fits your schedule first. See [how to leave a TikTok LIVE agency](/blog/how-to-leave-a-tiktok-live-agency/) if you are already signed with one.

## How to verify an agency in TikTok

TikTok registers approved networks on [LIVE Backstage](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en), which is a private operations portal for agencies rather than a public search directory for creators. Because there is no public TikTok directory of every LIVE Creator Network, verification happens inside the TikTok app itself.

1. **Open Agency Center** Go to Settings and privacy, Creator tools, LIVE Center, Agency Center, or open TikTok's [agency invite page](https://www.tiktok.com/tcn/agency_invite).
2. **Share your invitation code** A legitimate agency uses that code to send an invitation through TikTok's system.
3. **Check the sender name** Read the invitation screen carefully and confirm the network name matches the website and TikTok account you spoke with.
4. **Accept or decline in the app** TikTok's [invitation guide](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-agency-invitation) notes that you review the collaboration terms inside TikTok before deciding.
{: .steps}

Outside the app, verify who runs the network, where it is based, and whether its published results and creator accounts are real. Our guide on [whether TikTok LIVE agencies are legit](/blog/are-tiktok-live-agencies-legit/) covers the full verification steps.

## Red flags that rule an agency out

Skip any message or recruiter that shows these signs, no matter how big the network claims to be.

- **Asking for money** Any joining fee, deposit, or payment to unlock access or equipment.
- **Asking for your login** TikTok states it [will never ask for your account details](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/avoid-fraudulent-message-attacks-on-tiktok), and real networks never need your password or SMS codes.
- **Promised payouts or rankings** No agency controls TikTok's Gift algorithms, LIVE Fest leaderboards, or ban appeal outcomes.
- **Off-app onboarding only** Asking you to complete sign-up outside TikTok without ever sending an in-app Agency Center invitation.
{: .cards}

> **Heads up** Walk away if an agency refuses to put its fee, Diamond, and exit terms in writing before sending your invitation.
{: .callout .warn}
