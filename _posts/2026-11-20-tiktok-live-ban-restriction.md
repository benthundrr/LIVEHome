## Types of LIVE restriction

TikTok uses a range of actions on LIVE, from stopping a single LIVE to banning an account, and the right next step depends on which one you got.

TikTok's [Community Guidelines on LIVE](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features) and its [LIVE feature guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/live-feature-guidelines) describe these actions:

| What you might see | What TikTok says |
|---|---|
| Your LIVE is stopped | If a LIVE includes content that violates TikTok's policies, the session may be stopped |
| A temporary block on going LIVE | The creator could face temporary restrictions from going LIVE |
| Restricted LIVE features | TikTok may restrict the visibility of LIVE sessions or the use of certain LIVE features |
| Restricted monetization features | TikTok may temporarily restrict access to LIVE gifting, LIVE match and ranking features like Weekly Ranking |
| Guest features restricted | If a guest in a multi-guest LIVE breaks the rules, the creator may lose access to guest features temporarily |
| LIVE access revoked | TikTok may take stricter action, including revoking the ability to go LIVE |
| Account ban | Repeated or serious violations, including attempts to bypass restrictions, may result in an account ban |

TikTok also restricts LIVE sessions to viewers 18 and older when they contain content or behavior that isn't suitable for young people. That limits who can see your LIVE, and it's a different thing from losing access.

### How TikTok tells you

TikTok's LIVE feature guidelines say you get a notification in your in-app inbox that describes which feature was restricted, the reason and the period of time. A record is also kept in your system notifications.

If you're LIVE when it happens, TikTok says you also get a pop-up during your LIVE. While a feature is restricted, it may be greyed out or unavailable, and TikTok says it notifies you once access is restored.

## How to get unbanned on TikTok LIVE

Getting unbanned on TikTok LIVE goes through TikTok, and only TikTok can lift a LIVE ban or restriction. Here's the short version of what TikTok says to do, with the details in the sections below.

1. **Read your notice** TikTok's LIVE feature guidelines say your inbox notice describes which feature was restricted, the reason and the period of time.
2. **Wait out a temporary restriction** If the notice gives a period of time, TikTok says it notifies you once access is restored.
3. **Appeal if it was a mistake** TikTok says you can submit an appeal from your system notifications if you believe feature access was removed by mistake.
4. **Appeal a banned account** TikTok says to open the ban notification, tap Appeal and follow the instructions.
5. **Take policy training if offered** For certain first violations, TikTok says completing optional in-app policy training may lift eligible restrictions.
{: .steps}

## How to check what happened

Before you do anything else, find out exactly what TikTok restricted and why. Guessing makes it harder to write a clear appeal.

- **Read the notice** In the TikTok app, tap Inbox, then System notifications, then Account updates. TikTok's [content violations and bans page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/content-violations-and-bans) lists this as the place to review notices of violations.
- **Check your account status** TikTok's [account status page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/account-status) says you can go to Profile, tap Menu, select TikTok Studio and tap Account check under More tools. A checkmark means no issue and a warning sign means there's a problem.
- **Know what your status means** TikTok says your account status reflects your violation history over the last 90 days and can be Healthy, Fair, Severe or Banned.
- **Find the rule** Match the reason in your notice to the policy it names in the [Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features) so you know what TikTok thinks happened.
{: .cards}

> **Tip** TikTok says violations on your account expire after 90 days and are no longer taken into consideration. Keeping a clean record from here on matters.
{: .callout .tip}

## How appeals work

Appeals go through TikTok, and only TikTok decides the outcome. If you believe a decision was a mistake, here's the process TikTok describes.

1. **Open the notice** Go to Inbox, then System notifications, and find the notification about your restriction.
2. **Submit your appeal** TikTok's LIVE feature guidelines say that if you believe feature access was removed by mistake, you can submit an appeal from your system notifications.
3. **Appeal a banned account** If your whole account is banned, TikTok's [account safety page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/account-safety) says to open the ban notification, tap Appeal and follow the instructions.
4. **Wait for the decision** TikTok's [LIVE Studio FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/article/faqs-of-live-studio_en-US) lists three possible outcomes: the ban is lifted, it stays in place or the ban period is adjusted.
{: .steps}

Write your appeal plainly. Say what you were doing on your LIVE and why you think it didn't break the rule named in your notice.

> **Heads up** TikTok's LIVE Studio FAQ says you can only appeal a violation once. Read your notice carefully and get your explanation right the first time.
{: .callout .warn}

### Other things TikTok says can help

For certain first violations, TikTok says you may be offered optional in-app policy training. If you complete it, eligible restrictions on your account may be lifted.

TikTok also says that if your appeal is approved, your content and account status are reinstated. Deleting violative content doesn't remove the violation, so don't count on that as a fix.

## What an agency can and can't do

Be honest with yourself about what outside help can do here. TikTok decides every restriction, and TikTok reviews every appeal.

TikTok's guide [What Are TikTok LIVE Creator Networks?](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creator-networks/en/blog/tiktok-live-creator-networks-guide) describes agencies as third parties independent from TikTok. It says they may help creators better understand platform policies and account management practices.

| An agency can | An agency can't |
|---|---|
| Help you understand TikTok's policies | Lift a restriction or ban |
| Answer your questions about LIVE | Promise your appeal will succeed |
| Help you plan LIVEs that follow the rules | Get you around TikTok's restrictions |

> **Heads up** If anyone promises to get your LIVE ban removed, or asks for your password to fix it, walk away. TikTok says it may also restrict or ban accounts created to avoid a restriction or ban on another account you own.

## How to prevent it

The best way to keep your LIVE access is to know the rules before you go LIVE. Along with the Community Guidelines, TikTok says that to use LIVE to its full extent you must follow the [LIVE Feature Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/live-feature-guidelines) and the [LIVE Monetization Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/rules_and_guidance/live_monetization_guidelines).

### Behaviors TikTok names

TikTok's LIVE feature guidelines list behaviors that may affect your access to LIVE features, including:

- **Engagement tricks** Promises like 'like-for-like' or false incentives to get viewers to engage.
- **Fake engagement for Gifts** Asking for Gifts through overly repetitive activities, automatic systems or programmed loops.
- **Unoriginal content** Content that violates someone else's intellectual property or has no new or creative changes.
- **Pay-to-support and tragedy content** Presenting sensitive topics in an inflammatory way to push Gifts, or exploiting an ongoing tragedy for personal gain.
{: .cards}

### Habits that protect your access

- Stay authentic. TikTok says pretending to be someone else or streaming someone else's content without permission may lead to restrictions.
- Watch your guests. In multi-guest LIVEs, TikTok says a guest who breaks the rules can cost you guest features for a while.
- Manage your tools. TikTok says you're responsible for anything that happens on your LIVE, including through third-party tools like voice-to-text or on-screen comment displays.
- Disclose commercial content. TikTok says commercial content on LIVE must be disclosed with the commercial disclosure toggle.
- Check Account check regularly so a warning doesn't catch you by surprise.

TikTok's [LIVE requirements](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/what-is-tiktok-live) still apply too, including being at least 18 to go LIVE.
