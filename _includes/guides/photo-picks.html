{%- comment -%} Inline photos and the mobile cover for a guide post. Posts are ordered by filename (date, then slug). Rank in that list selects a pair from the 28-photo pool: photos 2*(rank mod 14) and the next one. Neighbouring posts therefore never share an inline photo, and across every 14 posts each photo is used once. The mobile cover is a third frame, offset so it is neither inline photo. team-dinner and creator-dinner stay on disk but are not in this pool. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign gp_pool = site.data.guide_photos.pool -%} {%- assign gp_half = gp_pool.size | divided_by: 2 -%} {%- assign gp_ordered = site.posts | sort: "path" -%} {%- assign gp_rank = 0 -%} {%- for gp_post in gp_ordered -%} {%- if gp_post.url == page.url -%} {%- assign gp_rank = forloop.index0 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign gp_pair = gp_rank | modulo: gp_half -%} {%- assign gp_i1 = gp_pair | times: 2 -%} {%- assign gp_i2 = gp_i1 | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign gp_cycle = gp_rank | divided_by: gp_half | modulo: 2 -%} {%- if gp_cycle == 0 -%} {%- assign gp_ci = gp_pair | plus: 3 | modulo: gp_half | plus: gp_half -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign gp_ci = gp_pair | plus: 8 | modulo: gp_half -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign gp_p1 = gp_pool[gp_i1] -%} {%- assign gp_p2 = gp_pool[gp_i2] -%} {%- assign gp_cover = gp_pool[gp_ci] -%}
